Molar Mass Calculator

Enter a chemical formula (e.g., H2O, CO2) to calculate its molar mass.

What is molar mass?

Molar mass is the mass of one mole of a substance, expressed in grams per mole (g/mol). It equals the sum of atomic masses of all atoms in the molecule.

How to use the calculator?

Enter the chemical formula using proper notation:

  • Element symbols: H, O, C, Na, Cl, etc.
  • Numbers after elements: H2O, CO2, CaCl2
  • Parentheses for complex compounds: Ca(OH)2, Al2(SO4)3

Examples:

  • H2O (water) = 18.016 g/mol
  • NaCl (salt) = 58.44 g/mol
  • CO2 (carbon dioxide) = 44.01 g/mol
  • CaCO3 (calcium carbonate) = 100.09 g/mol

Applications:

  • Stoichiometric calculations
  • Solution preparation
  • Laboratory work
  • Chemical analysis

Frequently Asked Questions

What is molar mass?
Molar mass is the mass of 1 mole of a substance, calculated in g/mol.
How to use the calculator?
Enter the chemical formula, for example NaCl, H2SO4.
What symbols can be used in the formula?
Use capital and lowercase Latin letters, for example Fe2O3 or Ca(OH)2. Parentheses and subscripts are supported.
Does the calculator handle complex formulas?
Yes, the calculator supports complex chemical formulas with multiple elements, parentheses, and coefficients.
What if I enter an unknown element?
The calculator will show an error message if the formula contains elements not in the database.
