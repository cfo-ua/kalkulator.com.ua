Online Average Calculator

The arithmetic mean is the sum of all numbers divided by their count. This calculator helps you quickly calculate the average value without errors and manual calculations.

How to use the calculator?

Simply enter numbers separated by commas (for example: 10, 20, 30), and click "Calculate". The calculator will instantly show the result.

Where is the arithmetic mean used:

  • In school: grades, tests, quizzes.
  • In statistics: data analysis, experiments.
  • In finance: average expenses, profit, investments.
  • In sports: average performance of a player or team.

Arithmetic mean formula:

Sum of all numbers / count of numbers. For example: (5 + 10 + 15) / 3 = 10

This tool is useful for students, teachers, financial analysts, and anyone working with numbers.

Frequently Asked Questions

How to calculate arithmetic mean manually?
Add all numbers and divide by their count. For example: (4 + 6 + 8) / 3 = 6.
Can I enter decimal or negative numbers?
Yes, the calculator supports both decimal fractions (e.g., 2.5) and negative numbers.
What if one of the numbers is 0?
Zero is counted as a full number. For example: the average of 0, 10, and 20 is (0 + 10 + 20) / 3 = 10.
What's the difference between arithmetic mean and median?
Arithmetic mean is the average value of all numbers. Median is the middle number in an ordered sequence.
Can I use this calculator for grades?
Yes, simply enter all your scores for a subject or semester, and the calculator will show your average grade.
What happens if I enter incorrect data?
Make sure all numbers are separated by commas, without letters. For example: 2, 4.5, -1 — correct. 2; 4; text — incorrect.
