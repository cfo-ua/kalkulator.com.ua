Frequently Asked Questions

What area units does the calculator support?
The calculator supports square meters (m²), square centimeters (cm²), hectares (ha), ares (a), square feet (ft²), square inches (in²), and acres.
How do I convert hectares to square meters?
Simply enter the hectare value, select 'hectares' as the source unit and 'square meters' as the target unit. 1 hectare equals 10,000 square meters.
What's the difference between an are and a hectare?
An are equals 100 square meters, while a hectare equals 10,000 square meters (100 ares). Hectares are commonly used for larger land areas.
