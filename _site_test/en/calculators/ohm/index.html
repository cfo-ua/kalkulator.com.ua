What is Ohm's Law?

Ohm's Law states that the current through a conductor is directly proportional to the voltage across it and inversely proportional to its resistance.

Ohm's Law Formula:

U = I × R

  • U = Voltage (Volts)
  • I = Current (Amperes)
  • R = Resistance (Ohms)

Applications:

  • Circuit design and analysis
  • Electrical troubleshooting
  • Power calculations
  • Component selection

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Ohm's law formula?
U = I × R, where U is voltage (V), I is current (A), R is resistance (Ω).
What can be calculated with this calculator?
Any of the three parameters if the other two are known.
What units should I use for input values?
U in volts (V), I in amperes (A), R in ohms (Ω).
Can I calculate power using Ohm's law?
Yes, power P = U × I = I²R = U²/R. You can derive power from any two known values.
