Frequently Asked Questions

Can I find out my exact age in days?
Yes, the calculator shows complete information: exactly how many years, months, and days have passed from your birth date to today's date.
What date format should I use?
Format: YYYY-MM-DD (for example, 2005-03-25). You can also select the date using the calendar picker.
Can I calculate a child's or baby's age?
Absolutely! The calculator is perfect for determining the age of infants and children — results show the exact number of months and days, which is crucial for pediatric care.
Can I use this calculator to check pension age eligibility?
Yes, many people use this tool to verify when they reach retirement age or to calculate years of service for pension applications.
Can I check my age on a specific date in the past or future?
Currently, the calculator shows age as of today's date. A future feature may include calculating age on specific dates.
Does the calculator work with future birth dates?
No, the birth date must be in the past. If you select a future date, an error message will appear.
Is this calculator accurate for legal documents?
Yes, the calculator provides precise age calculations suitable for official forms, legal documents, and administrative purposes.
How accurate is the leap year calculation?
The calculator accurately handles all leap years, including century years, ensuring precise age calculations regardless of when you were born.
