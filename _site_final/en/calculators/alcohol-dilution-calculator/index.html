Alcohol Dilution Calculator

Online Alcohol Dilution Calculator

🍷 This calculator helps you accurately determine how much water to add to an alcoholic beverage to achieve the desired alcohol by volume (ABV). A useful tool for home brewing, making tinctures, liqueurs, and cocktails.

🔬 How does alcohol dilution work?

When mixing alcohol with water, the volume percentage of alcohol decreases. The calculator uses mass balance formulas for precise proportion calculations.

📋 Calculator applications:

  • Home brewing: adjusting wine strength
  • Distilling: reducing distillate strength
  • Making tinctures: achieving optimal ABV
  • Cocktails: diluting strong spirits
  • Medical purposes: preparing alcohol solutions

⚠️ Important notes:

  • Use only drinking water or distilled water
  • Add water to alcohol, not vice versa
  • Dilution is an irreversible process
  • Temperature affects measurement accuracy

Frequently Asked Questions

How to properly dilute alcohol with water?
Always add water to alcohol, not the other way around. This allows better process control and prevents rapid alcohol evaporation.
What water should I use for dilution?
It's best to use distilled or soft drinking water without impurities. Hard water can worsen the taste of the beverage.
Does temperature affect dilution?
Yes, temperature affects alcohol density. For accurate calculations, all components should have the same temperature, preferably 20°C (68°F).
Can alcohol be diluted to any strength?
Yes, theoretically you can reduce the strength to any desired level, but practically people stop at 10-15% ABV to preserve taste qualities.
How to check the strength after dilution?
Use an alcoholometer or hydrometer to accurately measure the strength of the resulting beverage.
Does volume change when mixing?
Yes, when mixing alcohol with water, the total volume slightly decreases due to liquid contraction. The calculator accounts for this effect.
