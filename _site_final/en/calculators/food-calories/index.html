Understanding Nutrition Labels

Macronutrients (Macros)

  • Protein (4 cal/g): Essential for muscle repair and growth
  • Carbohydrates (4 cal/g): Primary energy source for body and brain
  • Fats (9 cal/g): Important for hormone production and vitamin absorption

Reading the Results

  • Total Calories: Energy content of your entire meal/recipe
  • Per Serving: Divide totals by number of servings
  • Macro Percentages: See the balance of protein/carbs/fat
  • Calorie Density: Calories per gram of food

Recipe Modification Tips

Reduce Calories Without Sacrificing Taste

  • Replace high-fat dairy with low-fat alternatives
  • Use cooking spray instead of oil for sautéing
  • Add more vegetables to increase volume without calories
  • Choose lean protein sources (chicken breast, fish, tofu)
  • Use herbs and spices instead of high-calorie sauces

Increase Protein Content

  • Add Greek yogurt to smoothies and dressings
  • Include legumes like beans and lentils
  • Use protein powder in baking recipes
  • Choose whole grains over refined grains
  • Add nuts and seeds for plant-based protein

Common Portion Sizes for Reference

  • Protein: 3-4 oz (85-113g) = palm-sized portion
  • Grains: 1/2 cup cooked = tennis ball size
  • Vegetables: 1 cup raw = baseball size
  • Fats: 1 tablespoon = thumb tip size
  • Fruits: 1 medium piece = tennis ball size
