Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are smart home energy savings estimates?
Estimates are based on typical usage patterns and device efficiency. Actual savings vary based on home size, energy rates, usage habits, and local climate conditions.
Which smart home devices provide the best ROI?
Smart thermostats typically offer the highest ROI (10-23% energy savings), followed by smart water heaters, LED lighting, and smart power strips for phantom load elimination.
Do smart home devices really increase property value?
Studies show smart home features can increase property value by 3-5%, with security systems and energy management providing the highest appeal to buyers.
What ongoing costs should I consider for smart devices?
Consider monthly subscription fees for cloud services, periodic software updates, battery replacements, and potential replacement after 5-10 years.
How do I calculate the value of convenience and time savings?
Assign an hourly value to your time (e.g., $25/hour) and estimate time saved monthly through automation, remote control, and automated scheduling.
Are there any hidden costs with smart home automation?
Potential costs include hub/controller purchases, professional installation, Wi-Fi upgrades, higher internet data usage, and learning curve time investment.
How long do smart home devices typically last?
Most quality smart devices last 5-10 years. Smart bulbs: 10+ years, thermostats: 8-10 years, security cameras: 5-7 years, depending on usage and quality.
Should I install smart devices myself or hire professionals?
Simple devices (plugs, bulbs) are DIY-friendly. Complex installations (thermostats, hardwired switches) may require electricians, affecting total cost calculations.
