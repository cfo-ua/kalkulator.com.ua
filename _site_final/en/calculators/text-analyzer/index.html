Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between letters and characters?
Letters count only alphabetic characters (A-Z, a-z), while characters include all symbols, numbers, punctuation, and spaces. Our analyzer shows both metrics for complete text analysis.
How does the word counter work?
Words are counted as text segments separated by whitespace (spaces, tabs, line breaks). Hyphenated words count as one word, and contractions (like 'don't') count as one word.
Can I analyze text in different languages?
Yes! The analyzer supports Unicode characters and works with text in any language. Letter counting recognizes alphabetic characters from various languages and scripts.
Is there a limit to how much text I can analyze?
There's no strict limit, but very large texts (over 1 million characters) might slow down analysis. The tool works best with typical documents, articles, and posts.
How accurate is the sentence counting?
Sentences are counted by detecting periods (.), exclamation marks (!), and question marks (?). The analyzer handles most standard punctuation patterns accurately.
Can I use this for SEO content optimization?
Absolutely! This tool is perfect for optimizing meta descriptions (155-160 characters), title tags (50-60 characters), and blog post length for SEO purposes.
Does the analyzer save my text?
No, all text analysis happens locally in your browser. Your text is never sent to servers or saved anywhere, ensuring complete privacy.
Can I analyze multiple paragraphs and documents?
Yes, paste entire documents with multiple paragraphs. The analyzer will count paragraphs as text blocks separated by line breaks.
Is this tool useful for academic writing?
Definitely! Students use this tool to meet essay word count requirements, track thesis progress, and ensure their writing meets assignment specifications.
How do I count characters for social media posts?
Simply paste your post content and check the 'Total Characters' count. This includes spaces and is perfect for Twitter (280 characters), Instagram captions, and other platforms.
