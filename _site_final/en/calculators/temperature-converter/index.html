Frequently Asked Questions

What temperature scales does the calculator support?
The calculator supports the three primary temperature scales: Celsius (°C), Fahrenheit (°F), and Kelvin (K).
How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?
Enter the Celsius value, select 'Celsius' as source and 'Fahrenheit' as target. The formula used is: (°C × 9/5) + 32 = °F.
What's the difference between Celsius and Kelvin?
Kelvin is the absolute temperature scale used in science. 0 K = -273.15°C. To convert: K = °C + 273.15.
