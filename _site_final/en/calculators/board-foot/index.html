What is a Board Foot?

A board foot is a unit of lumber volume equal to 144 cubic inches - a piece of wood 1" thick × 12" wide × 12" long.

Board Foot Formula:

Board Feet = (Thickness in inches × Width in inches × Length in feet) ÷ 12

Common Lumber Sizes & Board Feet:

  • 1×4 × 8': 2.67 board feet
  • 1×6 × 8': 4 board feet
  • 2×4 × 8': 5.33 board feet
  • 2×6 × 8': 8 board feet
  • 2×8 × 10': 13.33 board feet
  • 2×10 × 12': 20 board feet

Lumber Pricing by Type:

  • Construction lumber: $2-6 per board foot
  • Pine/SPF: $3-7 per board foot
  • Oak hardwood: $8-15 per board foot
  • Maple hardwood: $10-18 per board foot
  • Exotic hardwood: $15-50+ per board foot

Nominal vs Actual Lumber Sizes:

  • 1×4 nominal: actually 3/4" × 3.5"
  • 1×6 nominal: actually 3/4" × 5.5"
  • 2×4 nominal: actually 1.5" × 3.5"
  • 2×6 nominal: actually 1.5" × 5.5"
  • 2×8 nominal: actually 1.5" × 7.25"

Hardwood vs Softwood:

  • Softwood: pine, fir, cedar - construction, framing
  • Hardwood: oak, maple, cherry - furniture, flooring
  • Grading: affects price and quality significantly
  • Moisture content: kiln-dried vs air-dried pricing

Project Planning Tips:

  • Add 10-15% waste: for cuts, defects, mistakes
  • Buy from same lot: ensures color/grain consistency
  • Consider grades: select grade for visible surfaces
  • Acclimate lumber: store in project environment

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate board feet for a 2×4×8?
Board feet = (2 × 4 × 8) ÷ 12 = 5.33 board feet. Remember to use actual thickness and width, not nominal dimensions.
What's the difference between linear feet and board feet?
Linear feet measures length only. Board feet measures volume (thickness × width × length). A 10-foot 2×4 is 10 linear feet but 6.67 board feet.
Why is hardwood lumber more expensive?
Hardwood grows slower, requires more processing, and is graded more strictly. Premium hardwoods can cost 5-10× more than construction lumber.
Should I use nominal or actual dimensions?
Use actual dimensions for accurate calculations. A 2×4 is actually 1.5" × 3.5", which affects your board foot calculation.
How much waste should I factor in?
Add 10-15% for general projects, 20% for complex projects with many cuts, and 25% for beginner woodworkers.
Can I mix lumber from different suppliers?
Possible but not recommended for visible surfaces. Different suppliers may have slight variations in color, grain, and moisture content.
