Frequently Asked Questions

What credit score do I need for a small business loan?
Requirements vary: SBA loans typically need 680+, traditional banks prefer 700+, online lenders may accept 580+. Higher scores get better rates and terms.
How much revenue do I need to qualify for business loans?
Minimums vary by lender and loan type: SBA loans often require $100K+, online lenders may accept $50K+, traditional banks typically want $250K+ in annual revenue.
Can startups qualify for business loans?
Yes, but options are limited. Consider SBA microloans, equipment financing, or alternative lenders. Most require personal guarantees and may need collateral or strong personal credit.
What documents do I need for a business loan application?
Typically required: business and personal tax returns (2-3 years), bank statements (3-6 months), financial statements, business plan, and legal documents (articles of incorporation, licenses).
How does the debt-to-income ratio affect loan approval?
Lenders prefer debt-to-income ratios below 40-50%. This includes both business and personal debts. Lower ratios indicate better ability to service additional debt.
Can I get a business loan with bad credit?
Yes, but with limitations. Consider alternative lenders, revenue-based financing, or secured loans. Expect higher interest rates and may need a co-signer or collateral.
How long does the business loan approval process take?
Varies widely: online lenders (1-7 days), traditional banks (2-8 weeks), SBA loans (1-3 months). Preparation and complete documentation speeds the process.
What's the difference between secured and unsecured business loans?
Secured loans require collateral (equipment, real estate, inventory) and offer lower rates. Unsecured loans don't require collateral but have higher rates and stricter credit requirements.
