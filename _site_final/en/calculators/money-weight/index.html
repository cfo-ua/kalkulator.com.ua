Money and Currency Weight Calculator

Curious to know how much a million dollars weighs or your salary in banknotes? Our calculator will calculate the weight of any amount in different currencies and denominations.

Interesting facts about money weight:

  • US Dollar: all banknotes weigh the same — 1 gram
  • Million dollars: in $100 bills weighs ~22 lbs (10 kg)
  • Euro: weight depends on denomination — from 0.63g to 1.02g
  • British Pound: modern banknotes weigh from 0.73g to 0.94g

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a million dollars weigh in $100 bills?
A million dollars in $100 bills (10,000 banknotes) weighs approximately 22 pounds (10 kilograms).
Do all dollar bills weigh the same?
Yes, all US dollar banknotes regardless of denomination weigh approximately 1 gram.
What's the heaviest banknote in the world?
Euro banknotes vary in weight: €500 weighs 1.02g, while €5 weighs only 0.63g.
How much does a million British pounds weigh?
A million pounds in £50 notes (20,000 banknotes) weighs approximately 18.8 kg.
Does banknote condition affect weight?
Yes, worn banknotes can be 10-20% lighter due to material loss.
How much money can a person carry?
A person can carry ~44 lbs (20kg), equivalent to ~$2 million in $100 bills.
