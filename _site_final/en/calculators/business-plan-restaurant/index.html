Restaurant Business Plan Calculator

Planning to open a restaurant? Our calculator helps you analyze restaurant profitability, startup costs, and payback periods for your food service business investment.

What the Calculator Provides:

  • Startup Investment — kitchen equipment, furniture, renovation, licensing
  • Monthly Revenue — income projections from food and beverage sales
  • Operating Expenses — rent, salaries, food costs, utilities
  • Net Profit — monthly and annual profit projections
  • Payback Period — when your investment breaks even
  • ROI Analysis — return on investment metrics

Restaurant Business Advantages:

  • Higher Average Check — compared to cafés or fast food
  • Menu Diversity — opportunity for price differentiation
  • Additional Services — catering, events, delivery
  • Beverage Sales — high-margin alcoholic drinks
  • Special Events — celebrations, corporate functions
  • Brand Building — opportunity for franchise expansion

Restaurant Types & Average Checks:

  • Fast Casual — quick service dining ($8-15)
  • Casual Dining — family restaurants ($15-35)
  • Fine Dining — upscale establishments ($40-100+)
  • Ethnic Cuisine — specialized dishes ($12-30)
  • Steakhouses — meat-focused restaurants ($25-60)
  • Pizzerias — Italian cuisine ($10-25)

Key Restaurant Success Factors:

  • Concept — unique idea that differentiates your restaurant
  • Location — accessibility, parking, target demographics
  • Food Quality — talented chef, fresh ingredients
  • Service — professional waitstaff, timely delivery
  • Atmosphere — interior design, music, lighting
  • Marketing — online presence, reviews, events

Typical Restaurant Startup Costs:

  • Kitchen Equipment: $40,000-$150,000
  • Furniture & Interior: $25,000-$80,000
  • Renovation: $30,000-$120,000
  • Licenses & Permits: $5,000-$15,000
  • Monthly Rent: $5,000-$20,000
  • Staff Salaries: $8,000-$30,000 per month
  • Food Inventory: $4,000-$15,000 per month

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to open a restaurant?
Startup costs range from $150,000 to $400,000, including kitchen equipment ($40,000-$150,000), renovation ($30,000-$120,000), furniture ($25,000-$80,000), and working capital ($30,000-$50,000).
What's the average profit of a restaurant?
Average monthly profit ranges from $8,000-$35,000, depending on restaurant type, location, and average check size. Successful restaurants achieve 10-20% profit margins.
How long does it take for a restaurant to break even?
Typical payback period is 3-6 years. Fine dining establishments may take longer (4-7 years), while fast casual concepts may break even faster (2-4 years).
How many customers does a restaurant need daily?
For profitability, you need 60-200 customers per day depending on average check size and restaurant type. Average restaurant check ranges $15-50.
What are the main restaurant operating expenses?
Monthly expenses include: rent ($5,000-$20,000), staff salaries ($8,000-$30,000), food costs ($4,000-$15,000), utilities ($800-$2,000), marketing ($500-$2,000).
What licenses do I need for a restaurant?
Required: business registration, liquor license, food service permit, health department approval, fire department permit, music license. Total cost $5,000-$15,000.
What's the best location for a restaurant?
Ideal locations include: downtown areas, shopping centers, tourist zones, business districts. Key factors: foot traffic, parking availability, competition analysis.
How many staff members does a restaurant need?
Typical staff: 2-4 cooks, 3-8 servers, 1-2 bartenders, 1 manager. Total 8-20 employees depending on restaurant size and operating hours.
What's the optimal food cost percentage?
Target food cost is 28-35% of revenue. Alcoholic beverages should be 18-25%. Total food and beverage costs shouldn't exceed 35% of revenue.
