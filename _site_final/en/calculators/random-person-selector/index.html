Frequently Asked Questions

How random is the selection process?
The selector uses JavaScript's cryptographic random number generation, making selections truly random and unbiased. Each person has an exactly equal chance of being chosen.
Can I select multiple people at once?
Yes! You can choose to select 1, 2, 3, or more people simultaneously. This is perfect for forming teams or choosing multiple volunteers.
Can the same person be selected twice?
By default, once someone is selected they're temporarily removed from the pool. You can reset the selection to include everyone again for the next round.
How many names can I add to the selector?
There's no strict limit, but the tool works best with 3-100 participants. Very large groups might be better managed with multiple smaller selections.
Can I save my list of names for future use?
The names are saved in your browser's local storage, so they'll be there when you return. However, clearing browser data will remove the saved names.
Is this tool suitable for official contests or giveaways?
Yes! The random selection process is fair and transparent, making it suitable for contests, giveaways, and other situations requiring unbiased selection.
Can I use this for team formation in sports?
Absolutely! It's perfect for randomly assigning players to teams, choosing captains, or selecting starting lineups in recreational sports.
Does the tool work for classroom management?
Yes, teachers love using this tool to fairly select students for presentations, group work, answering questions, or classroom responsibilities.
Can I remove someone temporarily from selection?
Yes, you can easily remove names from the active pool temporarily and add them back later as needed.
Is there a way to weight the selection towards certain people?
No, this tool is designed for completely fair, unweighted random selection. Every participant has an exactly equal chance of being chosen.
