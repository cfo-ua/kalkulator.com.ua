Frequently Asked Questions

Is the generator algorithm fair?
Yes, we use a cryptographically secure random number generator that ensures equal chances for 'Yes' and 'No' answers.
Can I trust the results for important decisions?
The generator is suitable for light decisions or as an additional tool. For important decisions, always use thorough analysis and consultations.
What if the result doesn't satisfy me?
Your reaction to the result is valuable information about your true desires. Consider why the result disappointed you.
How often can I use the generator?
Use it as many times as you want. Each generation is independent and has the same probability of results.
Are my questions stored?
No, we don't store your questions. Everything happens locally in your browser and remains private.
Can I change the answer options?
Currently, standard 'Yes' and 'No' options are available. For other options, consider using our 'Decision Wheel'.
