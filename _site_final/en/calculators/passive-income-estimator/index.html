Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as passive income for this calculator?
Passive income includes rental properties, dividends, business ownership profits, royalties, peer-to-peer lending, and any income requiring minimal ongoing effort to maintain.
How realistic are the passive income projections?
Projections are based on your inputs and historical averages. Actual results vary based on market conditions, management quality, and economic factors.
What's the minimum investment needed to start building passive income?
This varies by stream. Dividend investing can start with $1,000, while real estate typically requires $20,000+ for down payments. Start with what you can afford.
How long does it take to build significant passive income?
Building substantial passive income typically takes 5-15 years of consistent investing and reinvestment, depending on your starting capital and contribution rate.
Should I focus on one passive income stream or diversify?
Diversification reduces risk. Start with one stream you understand well, then gradually add others as your knowledge and capital grow.
How do taxes affect passive income calculations?
Passive income is generally taxed differently than earned income. Consult a tax professional for specific advice, as rates vary by income type and jurisdiction.
What's the difference between passive and active income?
Passive income requires minimal ongoing effort once established, while active income requires continuous work. Examples: rental income vs. salary.
Can passive income really replace my full-time job?
Yes, with sufficient capital and time. Many achieve financial independence through passive income, but it requires disciplined saving, investing, and patience.
