Planning to travel, work, or study abroad? Our comprehensive visa eligibility calculator helps you assess your chances of obtaining visas for popular destinations including the United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Australia, and more.

How Does the Visa Eligibility Calculator Work?

Our calculator evaluates multiple factors that visa officers consider when reviewing applications:

  • Purpose of Visit: Tourism, business, work, study, or family visit
  • Financial Stability: Bank balance, income, and employment status
  • Travel History: Previous visa approvals and international travel
  • Ties to Home Country: Employment, property ownership, family connections
  • Duration of Stay: Short-term vs. long-term visits

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the visa eligibility calculator?
Our calculator provides estimates based on general visa criteria used by most countries. While it gives you a good indication of your chances, actual visa decisions depend on officer discretion and specific circumstances. Use it as a starting point for preparation.
Which countries are supported by this calculator?
The calculator covers major destinations including US (B1/B2, F1, H1B), Canada (visitor, study, work permits), UK (visitor, student, work), Australia (tourist, student, skilled), and Schengen countries. More countries may be added based on user demand.
What if my eligibility score is low?
A low score indicates areas for improvement. The calculator will suggest specific steps like building travel history, improving financial documentation, or strengthening home country ties. Don't be discouraged - many factors can be improved over time.
Do I need to provide real personal information?
No, this calculator doesn't store any data. All calculations are done in your browser. However, provide accurate information for the most reliable assessment.
How often should I retake the assessment?
Retake the assessment whenever your circumstances change significantly - new job, travel history, bank balance, etc. Your eligibility can improve over time as you build a stronger profile.
Can this calculator help with visa interview preparation?
Yes! The assessment highlights key areas visa officers focus on. Use the results to prepare documentation and practice explaining your travel purpose, financial situation, and home country ties.
What's the difference between visa types for the same country?
Each visa type has different requirements. Tourist visas focus on temporary visit intent, work visas require job offers and qualifications, student visas need educational acceptance. Choose the type matching your travel purpose.
How long does visa processing typically take?
Processing times vary by country and visa type: US (2 weeks to 6 months), Canada (2-12 weeks), UK (3-6 weeks), Australia (2-4 weeks). Apply well in advance and check current processing times on official websites.
