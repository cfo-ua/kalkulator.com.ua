Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the expiry date calculation?
The calculator provides precise date calculations based on your input production date and shelf life. However, actual freshness can vary based on storage conditions, so always check the product's appearance, smell, and taste.
Can I eat food after the expiration date?
This depends on the product type and how it's been stored. Some products may be safe for a few days past expiration, but always inspect the food carefully. When in doubt, it's safer to discard expired items.
What do the different color indicators mean?
Green indicates fresh products, yellow means attention needed (expiry approaching), and red indicates the product has expired. These visual cues help you quickly assess food safety status.
How should I store different types of products?
Dairy products should be kept refrigerated at 35-40°F (2-4°C), vegetables in cool places, meat at the coldest part of the refrigerator, and canned goods in cool, dry areas away from sunlight.
Does the calculator account for storage conditions?
The calculator shows standard shelf life based on optimal storage conditions. Poor storage conditions can significantly reduce actual shelf life, so always follow manufacturer recommendations.
What should I do with products nearing expiration?
Plan your meals to use items with the shortest shelf life first. Many products can be frozen to extend their usable life. Create a meal plan that prioritizes items close to expiration.
How can I reduce food waste effectively?
Use this calculator to track expiry dates, plan meals around expiration schedules, freeze suitable products before they expire, and follow proper storage guidelines to maximize freshness duration.
Are there different types of expiration dates?
Yes — 'Best Before' indicates quality may decline but food is often still safe, while 'Use By' is for safety and should be followed strictly. 'Sell By' is for retailers and doesn't indicate when food becomes unsafe.
