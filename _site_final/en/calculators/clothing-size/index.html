Breast Size & Clothing Size Calculator

Professional Breast Size & Clothing Size Calculator

Finding the right bra and clothing size shouldn't be guesswork! Our breast size calculator helps you determine your perfect bra size and clothing size across international sizing systems (US, EU, UK, International) based on your body measurements.

How to Use the Breast & Clothing Size Calculator

  1. Measure yourself: Use a soft measuring tape to get accurate measurements
  2. Enter measurements: Input your bust, underbust, waist, and hip measurements in centimeters
  3. Get your sizes: Receive bra size and clothing size recommendations for multiple international sizing systems

Measurement Guide for Bra Sizing

  • Bust: Measure around the fullest part of your chest while wearing a well-fitting bra, keeping the tape parallel to the floor
  • Underbust: Measure directly under your breasts, snugly but not tight
  • Waist: Measure around your natural waistline, typically the narrowest part of your torso
  • Hips: Measure around the fullest part of your hips, usually 7-9 inches below your waist

What You'll Get

  • Bra Size: Complete bra size with band and cup (e.g., 34B, 36C, 38DD)
  • International Clothing: XS, S, M, L, XL, XXL
  • European (EU): 34, 36, 38, 40, 42, 44, etc.
  • US Sizes: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, etc.
  • UK Sizes: 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, etc.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is this breast and clothing size calculator?
Our calculator uses standardized international size charts and provides accurate recommendations based on your measurements. It calculates both bra sizes and clothing sizes. However, sizes can vary between brands, so always check specific brand size charts when available.
How do I measure my bust and underbust correctly?
Measure your bust at the fullest part while wearing a proper-fitting bra. Measure underbust directly under your breasts, snugly but not tight. The tape should be parallel to the floor.
Can I use this calculator for both men's and women's clothing?
Yes! The calculator works for both men's and women's clothing, as it's based on body measurements rather than gender-specific sizing. For men, focus on the clothing size portion.
What if my measurements fall between sizes?
If you're between sizes, consider the type of fit you prefer. For a looser fit, choose the larger size. For a more fitted look, choose the smaller size. Also consider the fabric and style of the garment.
Why do bra sizes vary between different brands?
Bra sizes can vary significantly between brands due to different cup shapes, band materials, and sizing standards. Some brands use 'vanity sizing' while others run small. Always check each brand's specific size chart and read reviews about fit.
Can I use this for children's bra and clothing sizes?
This calculator is designed for adult sizing. Children's clothing and bras use different measurement standards and growth considerations.
How is bra cup size calculated?
Cup size is calculated as the difference between your bust and underbust measurements. Each 2.5cm (1 inch) difference typically corresponds to one cup size (A, B, C, D, etc.).
