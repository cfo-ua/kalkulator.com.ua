Frequently Asked Questions

What is a zodiac sign?
A zodiac sign is one of 12 astrological categories determined by the Sun's position at the time of birth. Each sign has its characteristic traits and influences personality according to astrology.
Why is it important to know your zodiac sign?
Your zodiac sign helps you better understand yourself, your tendencies, strengths, and can predict compatibility with other people. It's also useful for reading personalized horoscopes.
Does your zodiac sign change over the years?
In traditional Western astrology, no. It's based on a fixed system of Sun positions. However, in sidereal astrology (Indian tradition), there can be variations due to precession.
Can I find out someone else's zodiac sign?
Yes, simply enter their birth date in the form and the calculator will automatically show their zodiac sign.
Is this astrology or astronomy?
This calculator uses astrological data, which is a symbolic system for self-discovery and prediction, as opposed to scientific astronomy that studies celestial objects.
Are zodiac signs scientifically proven?
Astrology is not considered a science by the scientific community. However, millions of people find value in astrology for self-reflection, guidance, and understanding personality patterns.
What's the difference between sun sign and moon sign?
Your sun sign (calculated here) is based on your birth date and represents your core personality. Your moon sign requires birth time and location and represents your emotional nature.
Can zodiac signs predict the future?
While astrology enthusiasts believe signs can offer insights into tendencies and potential outcomes, it's best viewed as a tool for self-reflection rather than definitive future prediction.
