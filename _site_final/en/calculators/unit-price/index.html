Unit Price Calculator for Smart Shopping

Want to save money and always choose the best deals? Our calculator helps compare prices of different products and packages.

Why knowing unit price is important:

  • Save money: find the best deals
  • Smart choice: compare different brands and sizes
  • Budget control: plan expenses more accurately
  • Avoid marketing traps: large packages aren't always better value

Usage examples:

Food products: cereals, pasta, oil, dairy products

Household chemicals: detergents, shampoos, cleaning supplies

Office supplies: paper, pens, notebooks

Building materials: nails, paint, tiles

Units of measurement:

The calculator supports various units: grams, kilograms, liters, pieces, meters, and others.

Frequently Asked Questions

How to compare prices of products in different packages?
Enter price and quantity for each product. The calculator will show price per unit, and you can compare.
Are large packages always better value?
No, not always. Often manufacturers set higher unit prices for large packages, counting on buyer psychology.
Which products are most often compared by unit price?
Food products, household chemicals, office supplies, building materials — anything sold in different volumes.
How to consider expiration date when choosing?
If you won't use the large package before expiration, better take smaller one, even if unit price is higher.
Can you compare products of different brands?
Yes, but consider quality. Sometimes more expensive brand justifies higher price with better quality.
How to save using this calculator?
Before buying, calculate unit price for all options. This helps save 10-30% on purchases.
