Margin of Safety Calculator

Margin of safety is a key principle of value investing, introduced by Benjamin Graham. This calculator helps you determine safe purchase price for stocks and assess investment risks.

What is margin of safety:

  • Error protection - compensates for inaccuracies in valuation
  • Risk minimization - reduces probability of losses
  • Profit potential - provides room for growth
  • Psychological comfort - gives confidence in investments

Margin of safety recommendations:

  • 🟢 25-50% - ideal margin for conservative investors
  • 🟡 15-25% - acceptable margin for moderate investors
  • 🔴 Less than 15% - high risk margin, requires caution

Benefits of using:

  • 📊 Objective assessment - based on fundamental analysis
  • 🛡️ Risk management - helps avoid overpaying
  • 💰 Profit maximization - buying at favorable prices
  • 📈 Long-term strategy - building sustainable portfolio

Who is this for:

  • Value investors - followers of Graham-Buffett strategy
  • Conservative investors - priority on capital preservation
  • Long-term investors - oriented towards years and decades
  • Beginners - learning safe investment methods

Frequently Asked Questions

What is margin of safety in investing?
Margin of safety is the difference between intrinsic (fair) value of a stock and its current market price, expressed as percentage. The larger the margin, the safer the investment.
What is optimal margin of safety?
Benjamin Graham recommended minimum 25-30% for common stocks. Conservative investors often seek 40-50% margin of safety, especially during uncertain times.
Does margin of safety guarantee profit?
No, margin of safety doesn't guarantee profit, but significantly reduces risk of losses. It protects against valuation errors and unexpected market changes.
How to calculate intrinsic value?
Intrinsic value can be calculated using various methods: DCF analysis, P/E multiples, dividend model, asset analysis. Use our intrinsic value calculator for this.
What if margin of safety is negative?
Negative margin means the stock is overvalued. Better to abstain from buying or sell existing positions. Wait for better prices or consider other options.
