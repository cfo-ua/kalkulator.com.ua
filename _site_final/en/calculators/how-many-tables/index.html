How Many Tables Calculator

Event Table Calculator

Planning a wedding, corporate event, or celebration? Our calculator helps determine the optimal number of tables for comfortable seating of all guests.

How to use the calculator:

  1. Enter the total number of guests
  2. Choose table type (round or rectangular)
  3. Specify table size
  4. Click "Calculate"

Seating recommendations:

  • Round tables: create a more intimate atmosphere, easier to communicate
  • Rectangular tables: save space, suitable for formal events
  • Comfortable distance: 24-28 inches per person
  • Additional space: consider walkways between tables

Event types and features:

Weddings: typically use round tables for 8-10 people to create a cozy atmosphere.

Corporate events: rectangular tables for 6-8 people are more convenient for presentations and networking.

Birthdays: depends on age and celebration format.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many people can sit at a round table?
A round table with 60-inch diameter comfortably seats 6-8 people, 72-inch diameter seats 8-10 people.
What's the optimal number of people at a rectangular table?
A rectangular table 72x36 inches comfortably seats 6 people, 96x36 inches seats 8 people.
Should children be counted when calculating?
Yes, but children under 5 usually take less space. Count 2 small children as 1 adult.
How to account for service space?
There should be at least 48 inches between tables for free passage of waiters and guests.
What to do if the number of guests is odd?
Better to round up for comfort. One extra chair is not critical.
Can you mix different table types?
Yes, you can combine round and rectangular tables, but maintain the overall style of the event.
