What is a sphere?

A sphere is a perfectly round 3D shape where every point on the surface is equidistant from the center.

Sphere formulas:

  • Surface Area: A = 4πr²
  • Volume: V = (4/3)πr³
  • Diameter: d = 2r
  • Circumference: C = 2πr

Real-world examples:

  • Balls and spherical objects
  • Planets and celestial bodies
  • Bubbles and droplets
  • Architectural domes

Frequently Asked Questions

How to find sphere surface area?
Surface area of sphere: A = 4πr², where r is the radius.
How to find sphere volume?
Volume of sphere: V = (4/3)πr³, where r is the radius.
What units should I use for radius?
Enter radius in meters, centimeters, or any unit - results will be in corresponding square and cubic units.
What's the relationship between diameter and radius?
Diameter is twice the radius: d = 2r. So if you have diameter, divide by 2 to get radius.
