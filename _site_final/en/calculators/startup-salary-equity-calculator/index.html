Effective startup compensation planning enables you to:

Compensation Components Analyzed:

Base Compensation:

Equity Compensation:

  • Stock options: right to purchase shares at exercise price

  • Potential equity value: projected worth at exit scenarios

Frequently Asked Questions

How much equity should I offer to startup employees?
Typical ranges: Senior engineers (0.1-0.5%), Lead engineers (0.25-1.0%), VP-level (0.5-2.0%), C-level (1-5%). Early employees generally receive higher percentages. Adjust based on cash salary trade-offs and company stage.
What's a standard vesting schedule for startup equity?
The most common is 4-year vesting with a 1-year cliff. This means no equity vests for the first year, then 25% vests, followed by monthly vesting for the remaining 36 months.
How do I value equity for compensation purposes?
Use recent valuation (funding round or 409A valuation) as base case. Model conservative (50% discount), moderate (current value), and optimistic (2-5x growth) scenarios for total compensation calculations.
Should I offer more equity or higher salary?
Balance depends on company stage, cash position, and employee risk tolerance. Early-stage startups typically offer lower cash and higher equity. Later-stage companies can offer more competitive salaries.
How do I calculate the tax implications of equity compensation?
This calculator doesn't include tax advice. Consult a tax professional. Generally, stock options have tax implications at exercise and sale, while restricted stock is taxed at vesting.
What happens to equity when employees leave the company?
Unvested equity typically expires. Vested options usually must be exercised within 90 days of departure, though some companies extend this period. Include clear terms in equity agreements.
How does dilution affect employee equity value?
New funding rounds dilute ownership percentages but can increase per-share value. A smaller percentage of a more valuable company can be worth more than a larger percentage of a less valuable company.
What benefits should I include in total compensation?
Standard startup benefits: health insurance ($8,000-$15,000/year value), retirement contributions (3-6% of salary), equity, flexible time off, professional development, and workspace/equipment allowances.
