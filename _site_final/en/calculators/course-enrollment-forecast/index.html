Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are online course enrollment predictions?
Predictions are based on industry averages and typical success patterns. Actual results vary significantly based on course quality, marketing effectiveness, and market competition.
What factors most influence course enrollment success?
Course quality, instructor credibility, market demand, pricing strategy, and marketing effectiveness are the primary drivers of successful course enrollment.
How many students can I realistically expect in my first year?
New course creators typically see 50-500 students in their first year, depending on niche, marketing budget, existing audience, and course quality. Building slowly is normal.
What's a good course completion rate to aim for?
Industry average completion rates are 10-30%. Well-designed courses with good engagement can achieve 40-60% completion rates, which correlates with higher student satisfaction.
Should I launch on a marketplace or my own platform?
Marketplaces offer built-in traffic but lower profits and less control. Own platforms provide higher margins but require more marketing effort. Many creators use both strategies.
How do I price my online course competitively?
Research competitor pricing, consider your target audience's budget, factor in course length and depth, and test different price points. Value perception often matters more than absolute price.
What marketing strategies work best for course promotion?
Content marketing, email lists, social media, partnerships with other educators, free mini-courses, and webinars are most effective for organic course promotion.
How long should I make my online course?
Course length should match learning objectives, not arbitrary time goals. Most successful courses are 2-8 hours of content, broken into digestible modules with practical exercises.
