Sales Commission Calculator

Sales Commission Calculator Online

This calculator helps you calculate sales commission compensation for sales representatives and managers using different commission calculation schemes.

Types of commission schemes:

  • Fixed commission — constant percentage of all sales
  • Progressive commission — percentage increases when targets are met
  • Tiered commission — different percentages for different sales levels
  • Combined scheme — base salary + commission

Benefits of commission systems:

  • Motivates employees to increase sales
  • Links compensation to results
  • Helps control salary costs
  • Encourages achievement of target metrics

The calculator accounts for base salary, commission percentage, sales volume, and additional bonuses for accurate total compensation calculation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is sales commission?
It's compensation paid to a salesperson as a percentage of deals closed or number of products/services sold.
What types of commission schemes exist?
Fixed (constant percentage), progressive (increasing percentage), tiered (different percentages for different volumes), combined (salary + commission).
How to choose optimal commission percentage?
Depends on industry, product margins, sales complexity. Typical ranges: 1-3% for large deals, 5-15% for retail sales.
Should there be a minimum salary?
It's recommended to combine fixed portion (50-70%) with commission for employee income stability.
How to motivate plan achievement?
Use progressive scales — higher percentages for exceeding plans, bonuses for achieving key performance indicators.
How is commission calculated on returns?
Usually commission is deducted from salary when products are returned or deals cancelled, especially if due to salesperson's fault.
