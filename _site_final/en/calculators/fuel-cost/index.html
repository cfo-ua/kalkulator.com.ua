Fuel Cost Calculator

This online calculator allows you to calculate trip cost, fuel consumption, amount of fuel needed, or mileage. Enter two or three known values, and the calculator will automatically determine the others.

  • 🔁 Dynamic calculations: enter 2 or 3 values - get the rest
  • 📊 Supports all common scenarios: from expenses to budget planning
  • ✅ Simple to use and adaptive tool

How to Use This Calculator

Simply fill in any combination of known values:

  • Fuel consumption: How many liters per 100 km your vehicle uses
  • Distance: How many kilometers you plan to drive
  • Fuel price: Price per liter at the gas station
  • Fuel amount: Total liters of fuel used or needed
  • Total cost: Total amount spent on fuel

Common Use Cases

  • Trip Planning: Calculate how much fuel you'll need and what it will cost
  • Budget Management: Determine fuel expenses for your monthly budget
  • Fleet Management: Optimize fuel costs for business vehicles
  • Fuel Efficiency: Compare different vehicles' fuel consumption
  • Cost Analysis: Evaluate the true cost of driving vs. other transportation

Example Scenarios

  • "I'm driving 500 km, my car uses 8L/100km, fuel costs $1.50/L - what's the total cost?"
  • "I have $50 for fuel at $1.40/L - how many liters can I buy?"
  • "I used 45 liters for a 600 km trip - what's my fuel consumption?"

Perfect for personal use, business travel, logistics companies, taxi drivers, and anyone planning fuel expenses.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does this calculator work?
You can enter any three values (for example, consumption, mileage, and price), or just two if you only want to know the amount of fuel or cost. The calculator automatically determines what can be calculated and provides the answer.
Do I have to enter exactly three parameters?
No. If you only want to know how many liters of fuel you'll get for a specific amount - just enter two values: the amount and price per liter.
What happens if the data conflicts with each other?
If the entered values don't allow for correct calculation, the calculator will notify you and clear incorrect fields to avoid confusion.
Can I use this calculator for business analysis?
Yes. It's especially useful for logistics companies, fleet operators, taxi drivers, and anyone planning fuel expenses for business purposes.
Are decimal values supported?
Yes, you can enter values with decimal points - the calculator will automatically recognize them.
Are other expenses considered - like taxes?
No. The calculator is based solely on basic parameters - without considering taxes, service fees, or other additional costs.
What fuel consumption is considered good?
Typical passenger cars consume 6-12 L/100km. Compact cars: 5-8 L/100km, SUVs: 8-15 L/100km, trucks: 15-40 L/100km depending on load and conditions.
Can I calculate costs for electric vehicles?
This calculator is designed for liquid fuels (gasoline, diesel). For electric vehicles, you'd need to calculate based on kWh consumption and electricity rates.
