Compound Interest Calculator Online

This calculator helps you understand the power of compound interest and calculate financial results with regular contributions, compounding frequency, and investment timeline. See how your money can grow exponentially over time.

What the calculator includes:

  • Initial investment amount
  • Annual interest rate
  • Time period (in years)
  • Compounding frequency: monthly, quarterly, annually, etc.
  • Regular contributions: amount and frequency

Compound Interest Formula:

A = P × (1 + r/n)ⁿᵗ

  • P - principal amount (initial investment)
  • r - annual interest rate (as decimal)
  • n - number of times interest compounds per year
  • t - number of years
  • A - final amount

The calculator also accounts for regular contributions, which significantly increase the final amount through the compounding effect.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is compound interest?
Compound interest is interest calculated on both the initial principal and the accumulated interest from previous periods. It allows capital to grow exponentially over time, creating a 'snowball effect'.
What is the compound interest formula?
A = P × (1 + r/n)ⁿᵗ, where P is the principal, r is the annual rate, n is the compounding frequency per year, and t is the time in years.
What is interest compounding?
Compounding is the process of adding earned interest to the principal sum, so that the new total participates in the next interest calculation. More frequent compounding = higher returns.
Why consider regular contributions?
Regular contributions significantly increase the final amount, especially with long-term investing and compounding. Even small monthly additions can result in substantial wealth over time.
How does compound interest differ from simple interest?
Simple interest is calculated only on the principal amount, while compound interest is calculated on the growing total. This creates an 'interest on interest' effect that accelerates growth.
Can I see the results as a chart?
Yes, the calculator generates a growth chart based on your parameters. This allows you to visually assess the effectiveness of your investment strategy over time.
