Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are EPA range estimates for real-world driving?
EPA estimates are based on standardized testing. Real-world range typically varies ±20% depending on driving conditions, weather, and individual driving style.
Why does cold weather reduce EV range so much?
Cold temperatures reduce battery chemistry efficiency and require energy for cabin heating. Range can drop 20-40% in freezing conditions compared to optimal temperatures.
What's the difference between city and highway EV efficiency?
EVs are typically more efficient in city driving due to regenerative braking and lower speeds, opposite to gasoline cars which prefer highway driving.
How should I charge my EV for maximum battery life?
For daily use, charge to 80% and avoid letting battery drop below 20%. Only charge to 100% for long trips. Use Level 2 charging when possible.
What factors most significantly impact EV range?
Temperature, driving speed, terrain, and climate control use have the largest impacts. Highway speeds above 70 mph significantly reduce efficiency.
How do I plan a long-distance EV trip?
Use apps like PlugShare or ChargePoint to locate fast chargers every 150-200 miles. Plan stops at 20% remaining charge for safety buffer.
Can I tow with an electric vehicle?
Some EVs can tow, but it significantly reduces range (often 50%+ reduction). Plan accordingly with more frequent charging stops when towing.
How does EV efficiency compare to gasoline vehicles?
EVs convert about 85-90% of electrical energy to motion vs. 20-30% for gasoline engines, making them far more energy efficient overall.
