Online Internet Speed Test

Want to know how fast your internet is? This speed test helps measure your download speed in Mbps directly in your browser.

How Does the Test Work?

After clicking the button, we start downloading a test file from our server. Based on the time it takes, we calculate your download speed.

What You'll Get:

  • Download speed in megabits per second (Mbps)
  • Automatic connection quality assessment (low, medium, high)
  • Ability to repeat the test for reliability

Why Check Internet Speed?

  • To make sure your ISP is providing the promised speed
  • To find the cause of slow video streaming, Zoom calls, or online gaming
  • For optimizing router, Wi-Fi, or VPN settings
  • Before changing your internet plan or provider
  • To troubleshoot connectivity issues
  • For verifying network performance after upgrades

Speed Reference Guide:

  • 0-5 Mbps: Basic browsing, email, light social media
  • 5-25 Mbps: HD video streaming, video calls, online gaming
  • 25-100 Mbps: Multiple devices, 4K streaming, large downloads
  • 100+ Mbps: Heavy usage, smart home devices, professional work

Factors That Affect Speed:

  • Time of day: Peak hours may show slower speeds
  • Connection type: Wired connections are typically faster than Wi-Fi
  • Distance from router: Closer to router = better Wi-Fi speed
  • Device performance: Older devices may limit speed
  • Background activity: Other apps using internet reduce available speed
  • Network congestion: Many users on same network affects speed

The test works on both Wi-Fi and mobile networks. The more tests you run, the more accurate picture you'll have of your average speed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is internet download speed?
Download speed is the rate at which data is transferred from the internet to your device, measured in Mbps. It affects video streaming, file downloads, and website loading.
Why is it important to know internet speed?
To ensure your internet meets your needs for video, online learning, work, or gaming. It also helps troubleshoot slow connection issues and verify ISP service quality.
Why might results vary between tests?
Speed is affected by time of day, device type, Wi-Fi or cable connection, network congestion, background activity, router quality, or distance from router.
Is this test safe?
Yes, we use a simple server request without accessing your personal data. The test only measures the time to download a file.
Can I test speed on mobile?
Yes. The test works on mobile devices and tablets in any browser. Simply open the page and click the start test button.
What affects Wi-Fi speed?
Router age and quality, distance from router, walls and obstacles, interference from other devices, number of connected devices, and Wi-Fi standard (b/g/n/ac/ax).
Is download speed the same as upload speed?
No, this test measures download speed only. Upload speed (sending data) is typically slower and requires a separate test. Most internet activities rely mainly on download speed.
What should I do if my speed is slow?
Try restarting your router, moving closer to Wi-Fi, closing other apps, testing at different times, or contacting your internet service provider.
