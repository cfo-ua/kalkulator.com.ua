Frequently Asked Questions

How to interpret IQ test results?
IQ test measures intelligence quotient on a standard scale. The average score is 100 points. Results from 90-110 are considered normal, 110-120 above average, 120+ high.
What types of questions does the IQ test include?
The test includes questions on logical reasoning, pattern recognition, spatial thinking, number sequences, and verbal comprehension. All tasks are designed for objective intelligence assessment.
Can I retake the IQ test?
Yes! You can take the test multiple times. Retaking helps better understand task types and may show your progress in developing cognitive abilities.
How long does the IQ test take?
The test consists of 20 questions and typically takes 15-25 minutes. It's important to answer carefully and not rush with decisions.
Is this test as accurate as a professional IQ test?
While this test provides a good assessment of cognitive abilities, professional IQ tests are conducted by qualified psychologists. Our test is useful for self-assessment and understanding your strengths.
How can IQ test results be used?
Test results can be useful for understanding your cognitive abilities, choosing educational programs, career guidance, and self-knowledge. However, important decisions shouldn't be based on just one test.
Are my test results saved?
No, your results are displayed only to you and are not stored on our servers. The test is completely anonymous and confidential.
Is this test suitable for children?
The test is designed for people aged 16 and older. For younger children, there are specialized tests adapted to their developmental level.
Does IQ reflect all aspects of intelligence?
IQ shows abilities for logical thinking and problem-solving, but doesn't reflect creativity, emotional intelligence, motivation, and other important aspects of human mind.
Is the test adapted for English speakers?
Yes, our test is adapted for English-speaking users and considers cultural specifics when formulating tasks.
