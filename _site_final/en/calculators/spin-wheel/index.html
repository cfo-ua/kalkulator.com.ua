Frequently Asked Questions

How many options can I add to the wheel?
There's no technical limit, but we recommend up to 20-30 options for optimal visualization. With too many options, text may become difficult to read.
Is the selection algorithm truly fair?
Yes, we use a cryptographically secure random number generator. Each option has equal chances of being selected regardless of sector size.
What does 'eliminate selected option' mean?
When enabled, the selected option will be removed from the wheel after each spin. This is useful for gradual elimination of choices.
Are my options saved between visits?
Yes, your option list is saved locally in your browser. You'll see your last options when you return to the page.
Can I change the sector colors?
Colors are generated automatically for better visual appeal. We plan to add color customization options in the future.
How do I reset all options?
Use the 'Clear All' button or remove options individually using the ❌ buttons next to each option.
Can I use emojis in options?
Yes! Emojis and special characters are fully supported and make the wheel more visually appealing.
What if the wheel spins too fast?
Spin speed is randomized for greater unpredictability. The wheel will always stop within 3-5 seconds.
Can I use this for official contests?
Our wheel is perfect for casual games and informal contests. For official competitions, consider additional verification methods.
Is there a limit to text length for options?
Options are limited to 50 characters each. Longer text will be automatically truncated with '...' for display.
