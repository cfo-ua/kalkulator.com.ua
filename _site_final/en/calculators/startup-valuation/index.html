Startup Valuation Calculator - Multi-Method Company Valuation

Estimate your startup valuation using multiple proven methodologies. This comprehensive tool helps entrepreneurs, investors, and advisors determine fair company value for fundraising, partnerships, and strategic planning.

Valuation Methods Included:

  • Revenue Multiple Method - Based on industry-standard revenue multiples
  • Cost Approach - Sum of investments and development costs

  • Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) - Future cash flow projections
  • Risk-Adjusted NPV - Net present value with startup risk factors

Valuation Methodology Insights:

Revenue Multiple: Most common for SaaS and tech startups with recurring revenue. Multiples typically range from 2-15x depending on growth rate, margins, and market dynamics.

Cost Approach: Useful for early-stage startups with significant R&D investment. Considers all capital invested plus development costs and risk adjustments.

Market Comparable: Benchmarks against similar companies that have been acquired or funded recently. Requires careful selection of truly comparable businesses.

Important Considerations:

  • Industry variations - Valuation multiples vary significantly by sector
  • Market conditions - Economic climate affects all valuation methods
  • Unique factors - Consider proprietary technology, team, and market position

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between pre-money and post-money valuation?
Pre-money valuation is the company's value before investment. Post-money valuation is pre-money plus the investment amount. For example, $5M pre-money + $2M investment = $7M post-money.
Which valuation method is most accurate for startups?
No single method is universally accurate. Revenue multiples work well for recurring revenue businesses, while cost approach suits early-stage companies. Use multiple methods and consider the range.
How often should I revalue my startup?
Revalue quarterly or after significant milestones like product launches, major customer wins, funding rounds, or market changes. Regular valuation tracking helps with strategic planning.
What revenue multiple should I use for my industry?
SaaS: 5-15x, E-commerce: 2-5x, Marketplaces: 8-20x, Hardware: 1-3x, Biotech: 3-8x. These vary significantly based on growth rate, margins, and market conditions.
How do I value a pre-revenue startup?
For pre-revenue startups, focus on cost approach (total investment + risk premium), market comparables, or DCF based on projected revenues. Consider team, technology, and market size.
Should I include IP and intangible assets in valuation?
Yes, intellectual property, brand value, customer relationships, and proprietary technology should be considered, especially in the cost approach and market comparable methods.
How do market conditions affect startup valuations?
Market conditions significantly impact valuations. Bull markets increase multiples and risk tolerance, while bear markets reduce valuations and emphasize profitability over growth.
Can this calculator be used for established businesses?
This calculator is optimized for startups and early-stage companies. Established businesses may require more sophisticated valuation methods and professional appraisal.
