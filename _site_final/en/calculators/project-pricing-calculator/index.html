Frequently Asked Questions

How do I establish baseline costs for project pricing?
Track historical projects by type, hours spent, and outcomes. Use your best similar project as baseline, then adjust for complexity, timeline, and scope differences.
What complexity factors should I consider?
Technical difficulty, number of stakeholders, integration requirements, custom features, regulatory compliance, and client approval processes all affect complexity.
How much should I charge for rush projects?
Rush projects typically warrant 25-100% premium depending on timeline compression. Factor in overtime work, opportunity cost, and stress levels.
Should I include revisions in my project price?
Include 2-3 rounds of reasonable revisions in base price. Clearly define what constitutes a revision vs scope change, and price additional revisions separately.
How do I handle scope creep in fixed-price projects?
Define scope clearly upfront, include change order process in contract, and charge separately for scope additions. Build 10-20% buffer into initial quote.
What's the difference between project and hourly pricing?
Project pricing focuses on deliverable value and allows profit from efficiency. Hourly pricing ties income directly to time spent, limiting scalability.
How do I price projects I've never done before?
Research similar projects, break into smaller components you understand, add higher risk buffer (25-50%), and consider charging discovery phase separately.
Should I offer payment terms for larger projects?
Yes, typically 25-50% upfront, milestone payments during project, and final payment on completion. This improves cash flow and reduces risk.
