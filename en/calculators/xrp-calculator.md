    <h3>About XRP (Ripple):</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Purpose:</strong> Digital payment network for financial institutions</li>

      <li><strong>Speed:</strong> 3-5 second transaction settlement</li>
      <li><strong>Supply:</strong> 100 billion XRP maximum supply</li>

    </ul>

    <h3>XRP Investment Strategies:</h3>
    <p><strong>Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA):</strong></p>
    <ul>
      <li>Invest fixed amount regularly regardless of price</li>
      <li>Reduces impact of volatility over time</li>
      <li>Good for long-term XRP accumulation</li>
      <li>Helps avoid timing the market</li>
    </ul>

    <p><strong>Lump Sum Investment:</strong></p>
    <ul>
      <li>Invest all capital at once</li>
      <li>Higher potential returns in bull markets</li>
      <li>More risk if timing is poor</li>
      <li>Requires market timing skills</li>
    </ul>

    <p><strong>Value Averaging:</strong></p>
    <ul>
      <li>Adjust investment to reach target portfolio value</li>
      <li>Buy more when price is low, less when high</li>
      <li>More active than DCA strategy</li>
      <li>Can outperform DCA in volatile markets</li>
    </ul>
