    <h3>Understanding Markup vs Margin</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Margin</strong>  -  profit as a percentage of selling price</li>
      <li><strong>Markup</strong>  -  profit as a percentage of cost price</li>
    </ul>

    <p>While often confused, the formulas are different:</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Margin = (Selling Price − Cost) ÷ Selling Price × 100%</strong></li>
      <li><strong>Markup = (Selling Price − Cost) ÷ Cost × 100%</strong></li>
    </ul>

      <li><strong>Selling Price + Margin %</strong> → Calculate cost, markup, and profit</li>
      <li><strong>Selling Price + Markup %</strong> → Determine cost, margin, and profit</li>
    </ul>
