    <h3>Key Technical Indicators Explained:</h3>
    <p><strong>RSI (Relative Strength Index):</strong></p>
    <ul>
      <li>Range: 0-100, with 70+ indicating overbought conditions</li>
      <li>Below 30 suggests oversold conditions and potential buying opportunity</li>
      <li>Divergences between price and RSI can signal trend reversals</li>
      <li>Most effective in ranging markets rather than strong trends</li>
    </ul>

    <p><strong>MACD Analysis:</strong></p>
    <ul>
      <li>Signal line crossovers indicate potential trend changes</li>
      <li>Histogram shows momentum strength and direction</li>
      <li>Bullish when MACD line crosses above signal line</li>
      <li>Bearish when MACD line crosses below signal line</li>
    </ul>

    <p><strong>Moving Averages:</strong></p>
    <ul>
      <li>20-day MA: Short-term trend direction</li>
      <li>50-day MA: Medium-term trend assessment</li>
      <li>200-day MA: Long-term trend identification</li>
      <li>Golden Cross (50 MA > 200 MA): Bullish signal</li>
      <li>Death Cross (50 MA < 200 MA): Bearish signal</li>
    </ul>
