    <h3>VAT Calculation Formulas</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Price with VAT =</strong> Price without VAT × (1 + rate / 100)</li>
      <li><strong>Price without VAT =</strong> Price with VAT ÷ (1 + rate / 100)</li>
      <li><strong>VAT Amount =</strong> Price with VAT - Price without VAT</li>
      <li><strong>VAT Rate =</strong> ((Price with VAT ÷ Price without VAT) - 1) × 100%</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Understanding VAT (Value Added Tax)</h3>
    <p><strong>VAT</strong> is an indirect tax applied to goods and services at each stage of the supply chain. Common VAT rates include:</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Standard rates:</strong> 15-25% (most common: 20-21%)</li>
      <li><strong>Reduced rates:</strong> 5-10% (essential goods, food, books)</li>
      <li><strong>Zero rate:</strong> 0% (exports, certain medical supplies)</li>
      <li><strong>Exempt:</strong> No VAT (financial services, insurance)</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>VAT Calculation Examples</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Adding VAT:</strong> Price $100, VAT 20% ⇒ Final price = $100 × 1.2 = $120</li>
      <li><strong>Removing VAT:</strong> Price $120, VAT 20% ⇒ Net price = $120 ÷ 1.2 = $100, VAT = $20</li>
      <li><strong>Finding VAT rate:</strong> Net $100, Gross $125 ⇒ Rate = ((125 ÷ 100) - 1) × 100% = 25%</li>
    </ul>
