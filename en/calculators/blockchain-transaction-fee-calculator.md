    <h2>Blockchain Transaction Fee Calculator</h2>
    <p>Calculate optimal transaction fees across multiple blockchain networks with our comprehensive <strong>blockchain transaction fee calculator</strong>. Estimate gas costs, compare networks, and find the best times for cryptocurrency transactions to minimize fees.</p>

    <h3>💰 How Blockchain Fees Work</h3>
    <p>Blockchain transaction fees compensate network validators and miners for processing transactions:</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>⛽ Gas Fees:</strong> Computational cost of executing transactions</li>
      <li><strong>🏃 Priority Fees:</strong> Additional payment for faster confirmation</li>
      <li><strong>📊 Network Congestion:</strong> Higher demand increases fees</li>
      <li><strong>⚖️ Transaction Complexity:</strong> Smart contracts cost more than simple transfers</li>
      <li><strong>🕐 Timing:</strong> Fees fluctuate based on network usage patterns</li>
      <li><strong>🔄 Confirmation Speed:</strong> Higher fees = faster confirmation</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>🌐 Major Blockchain Networks</h3>
    
    <h4>₿ Bitcoin (BTC):</h4>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Fee Model:</strong> Satoshis per byte (sat/vB)</li>
      <li><strong>Average Fee:</strong> $1-50 (depends on congestion)</li>
      <li><strong>Confirmation Time:</strong> 10-60 minutes</li>
      <li><strong>Peak Hours:</strong> Business hours in major markets</li>
      <li><strong>Optimization:</strong> SegWit, batching, low-priority timing</li>
    </ul>

    <h4>Ξ Ethereum (ETH):</h4>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Fee Model:</strong> Gas price × Gas limit (Gwei)</li>
      <li><strong>Average Fee:</strong> $5-100 (highly variable)</li>
      <li><strong>Confirmation Time:</strong> 15 seconds - 5 minutes</li>
      <li><strong>Peak Hours:</strong> US/Europe daytime</li>
      <li><strong>Optimization:</strong> EIP-1559, Layer 2 solutions</li>
    </ul>

    <h4>🔷 Layer 2 Solutions:</h4>

      <li><strong>Optimism:</strong> Fast finality, low costs</li>
      <li><strong>Lightning Network:</strong> Near-zero Bitcoin fees</li>

faq:
  - question: Why are blockchain transaction fees so volatile?
    answer: "Fees are determined by supply and demand. When many people want to transact simultaneously (high demand) but block space is limited (fixed supply), fees increase. Network congestion, DeFi activity, NFT drops, and market volatility all affect demand."
  - question: What's the difference between gas price and gas limit?
    answer: "Gas limit is the maximum computational work your transaction can use. Gas price is how much you pay per unit of gas. Total fee = gas used × gas price. Setting a higher gas price makes miners prioritize your transaction."
  - question: When are the best times to make blockchain transactions?
    answer: "Generally weekends and late night/early morning hours (UTC) have lower fees. Avoid major market events, DeFi yield farming updates, popular NFT launches, and times when both US and European markets are active."
  - question: Should I use Layer 2 solutions to save on fees?
    answer: "For frequent transactions or small amounts, Layer 2 solutions like Polygon, Arbitrum, or Lightning Network offer significant savings (90%+ fee reduction). However, moving funds to/from Layer 2 requires mainnet transactions."
  - question: What happens if I set the gas price too low?
    answer: "Your transaction may stay pending for hours, days, or until dropped from the mempool. You can speed up by sending a replacement transaction with higher fees, or cancel by sending 0 ETH to yourself with higher fees and the same nonce."
  - question: Are failed transactions still charged fees?
    answer: "Yes, failed transactions still consume gas and you pay fees because the network processed the computation until failure. This is why it's important to test smart contract interactions with small amounts first."
  - question: How do I estimate fees for smart contract interactions?
    answer: "Use your wallet's simulation feature or blockchain explorers to estimate gas usage. Complex DeFi operations often need 150,000-500,000 gas. Start with conservative estimates and adjust based on actual usage."
  - question: What are MEV and priority fees?
    answer: "MEV (Maximal Extractable Value) is profit from reordering transactions. Priority fees incentivize validators to include your transaction faster. In competitive environments (like arbitrage), higher priority fees increase success chances."
