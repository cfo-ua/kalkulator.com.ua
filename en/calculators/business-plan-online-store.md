    <h2>Online Store Business Plan Calculator</h2>
    <p>Planning to launch an <strong>online store or e-commerce business</strong>? Our calculator helps you analyze <strong>e-commerce profitability</strong>, startup costs, and payback periods for your digital commerce venture.</p>

    <ul>
      <li><strong>Startup Investment</strong> — website development, inventory, marketing</li>
      <li><strong>Monthly Sales</strong> — traffic, conversion, average order value</li>
      <li><strong>Operating Expenses</strong> — fulfillment, logistics, advertising</li>
      <li><strong>Net Profit</strong> — monthly and annual profit projections</li>

      <li><strong>E-commerce Metrics</strong> — CAC, LTV, ROAS analysis</li>

    <h3>E-commerce Business Advantages:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Global Reach</strong> — access to worldwide customer base</li>
      <li><strong>Low Overhead</strong> — compared to traditional retail stores</li>
      <li><strong>Scalability</strong> — easy to expand product lines and markets</li>
      <li><strong>Automation</strong> — reduce manual processes and costs</li>
      <li><strong>Data Analytics</strong> — detailed customer and sales insights</li>
      <li><strong>24/7 Operations</strong> — sales continue around the clock</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Types of E-commerce Models:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Traditional E-commerce</strong> — own inventory and fulfillment</li>
      <li><strong>Dropshipping</strong> — sell without holding inventory</li>
      <li><strong>Marketplace Selling</strong> — Amazon, eBay, Etsy platforms</li>
      <li><strong>Subscription Commerce</strong> — recurring revenue model</li>
      <li><strong>Digital Products</strong> — courses, software, content</li>
      <li><strong>B2B E-commerce</strong> — business-to-business sales</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Key Success Factors:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Quality Website</strong> — user experience, speed, mobile optimization</li>
      <li><strong>SEO & Marketing</strong> — driving targeted traffic</li>
      <li><strong>Conversion Optimization</strong> — maximizing sales from visitors</li>
      <li><strong>Logistics Excellence</strong> — fast and reliable fulfillment</li>
      <li><strong>Customer Service</strong> — support, returns, satisfaction</li>
      <li><strong>Data-Driven Decisions</strong> — analytics and optimization</li>
    </ul>

    <ul>
      <li><strong>Website Development:</strong> $2,000-$15,000</li>
      <li><strong>Initial Inventory:</strong> $10,000-$50,000</li>
      <li><strong>Monthly Marketing:</strong> $1,000-$10,000</li>
      <li><strong>Operating Costs:</strong> $500-$3,000/month</li>
      <li><strong>Average Order Value:</strong> $30-$150</li>
      <li><strong>Conversion Rate:</strong> 1-5%</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Traffic Sources:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>SEO</strong> — organic search (free long-term traffic)</li>
      <li><strong>Google Ads</strong> — paid search advertising</li>
      <li><strong>Social Media</strong> — Facebook, Instagram, TikTok ads</li>
      <li><strong>Email Marketing</strong> — customer retention and repeat sales</li>
      <li><strong>Affiliate Programs</strong> — partner-driven sales</li>
      <li><strong>Marketplaces</strong> — Amazon, eBay, platform traffic</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Key Performance Metrics:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Conversion Rate</strong> — percentage of visitors who purchase</li>
      <li><strong>Average Order Value (AOV)</strong> — average purchase amount</li>
      <li><strong>Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)</strong> — total customer value</li>
      <li><strong>Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)</strong> — cost to acquire customer</li>
      <li><strong>Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)</strong> — advertising effectiveness</li>
      <li><strong>Repeat Purchase Rate</strong> — customer retention metric</li>
    </ul>

    <ul>
      <li><strong>Holiday Season</strong> — peak sales period (up to 300% increase)</li>
      <li><strong>Black Friday/Cyber Monday</strong> — highest sales days</li>
      <li><strong>Summer Slowdown</strong> — 20-30% decrease in some categories</li>
      <li><strong>Valentine's Day</strong> — gift category boost</li>
      <li><strong>Back-to-School</strong> — August-September opportunity</li>
    </ul>

    <ul>
      <li><strong>A/B Testing</strong> — optimize product pages and checkout</li>
      <li><strong>Upselling/Cross-selling</strong> — increase average order value</li>
      <li><strong>Email Campaigns</strong> — recover abandoned carts</li>
      <li><strong>Loyalty Programs</strong> — encourage repeat purchases</li>
      <li><strong>Dynamic Pricing</strong> — adjust prices based on demand</li>
      <li><strong>Product Bundling</strong> — increase order size</li>
    </ul>
