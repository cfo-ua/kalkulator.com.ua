    answer: "Material quality and project complexity are the largest factors. Premium materials can double costs, while project modifications during construction add significant expenses."
  - question: "How do I decide between DIY and hiring a contractor?"
    answer: "Consider your skill level, available time, tool costs, and project complexity. DIY typically saves 40-60% on labor but requires significant time investment."
  - question: "What tools should I buy vs. rent for DIY projects?"
    answer: "Buy tools you'll use frequently (drill, saw, basic hand tools). Rent expensive specialty tools (tile saw, floor sanders) needed for single projects."
  - question: "How can I find the best prices on materials?"
    answer: "Compare prices at multiple stores, check online retailers, look for seasonal sales, and consider contractor-grade suppliers for bulk purchases."
  - question: "What mistakes increase DIY project costs the most?"

  - question: "How do I calculate material waste for different projects?"
