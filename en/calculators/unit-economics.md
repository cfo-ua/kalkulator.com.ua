    <h2>Unit Economics Calculator</h2>
    <p><strong>Unit economics</strong> is a financial analysis method that evaluates business profitability at the individual product or service level. It's a crucial tool for entrepreneurs, startups, and established businesses to assess financial viability and growth potential.</p>
    
    <p>This online calculator helps you quickly evaluate your business financial model: determine if selling your product is profitable, how many units you need to sell to cover costs, and what profit to expect at given sales volumes.</p>
    
    <h3>What Unit Economics Reveals:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Contribution margin per unit</strong>  -  profit generated by each sale</li>
      <li><strong>Contribution margin percentage</strong>  -  profitability ratio</li>
      <li><strong>Break-even point</strong>  -  units needed to cover all costs</li>
      <li><strong>Monthly profit projection</strong>  -  expected earnings at target volume</li>
      <li><strong>12-month cumulative profit</strong>  -  long-term profitability forecast</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Key Unit Economics Formulas:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Contribution Margin = Unit Price - Variable Cost per Unit</strong></li>
      <li><strong>Contribution Margin % = (Contribution Margin ÷ Unit Price) × 100%</strong></li>
      <li><strong>Break-Even Point = Fixed Costs ÷ Contribution Margin</strong></li>
      <li><strong>Monthly Profit = (Contribution Margin × Monthly Units) - Fixed Costs</strong></li>
    </ul>
