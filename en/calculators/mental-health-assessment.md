    <ul>

      <li><strong>😰 Anxiety & Worry:</strong> Generalized anxiety, panic symptoms, social anxiety</li>
      <li><strong>😤 Stress Management:</strong> Stress tolerance, coping mechanisms, overwhelm</li>
      <li><strong>😴 Sleep & Energy:</strong> Sleep quality, fatigue levels, energy patterns</li>
      <li><strong>👥 Social Functioning:</strong> Relationships, social interaction, isolation</li>

      <li><strong>🤝 Social Connectedness:</strong> Strong support networks and healthy relationships</li>
      <li><strong>💪 Self-Efficacy:</strong> Confidence in personal ability to handle challenges</li>
      <li><strong>☀️ Optimism:</strong> Positive outlook and hope for the future</li>
      <li><strong>🎨 Purpose & Meaning:</strong> Clear sense of life purpose and values</li>
      <li><strong>⚖️ Stress Management:</strong> Effective coping strategies and stress tolerance</li>

    <ul>
      <li>Persistent sadness, hopelessness, or emptiness lasting weeks</li>
      <li>Excessive worry, fear, or anxiety interfering with daily life</li>
      <li>Significant changes in appetite, weight, or sleep patterns</li>
      <li>Loss of interest in activities once enjoyed</li>
      <li>Difficulty concentrating, remembering, or making decisions</li>
      <li>Thoughts of death, suicide, or self-harm</li>
      <li>Substance use to cope with emotions</li>
      <li>Social withdrawal or isolation from others</li>
      <li>Inability to bounce back from setbacks or stress</li>
    </ul>

  - question: What's the difference between mental wellness and resilience?
    answer: "Mental wellness measures your current psychological state (mood, anxiety, functioning), while resilience measures your ability to cope with and recover from challenges. Both are important for overall mental health."
  - question: Can this assessment diagnose depression or anxiety?
    answer: "No, this is a screening tool, not a diagnostic instrument. Only qualified mental health professionals can provide clinical diagnoses. However, the assessment can help identify symptoms that warrant professional evaluation."
  - question: What should I do if I score low on this assessment?
    answer: "Low scores suggest you may benefit from mental health support. Consider speaking with a counselor, therapist, or your healthcare provider. If you're having thoughts of self-harm, seek immediate professional help."
  - question: How can I improve my mental wellness and resilience scores?
    answer: "Focus on evidence-based strategies like regular exercise, mindfulness practice, quality sleep, social connection, and stress management. Consider therapy or counseling for personalized strategies. Small, consistent changes often lead to significant improvements."
  - question: Is this assessment suitable for teenagers?
    answer: "This assessment is designed for adults 18+. Teenagers have different developmental considerations and should use age-appropriate tools under professional guidance. Parents concerned about teen mental health should consult healthcare providers."
  - question: How does stress affect mental wellness and resilience?
    answer: "Chronic stress can negatively impact both mental wellness (increasing anxiety and depression symptoms) and resilience (reducing coping abilities). Managing stress through healthy coping strategies is crucial for maintaining good mental health."
  - question: Can medication affect my assessment results?
    answer: "Yes, psychiatric medications, as well as some other medications, can affect mood and cognitive function. If you're taking medication, consider this when interpreting results and discuss findings with your healthcare provider."

    answer: "Some variation is normal as mental health fluctuates with life circumstances. Significant changes might reflect real improvements or challenges, medication changes, or major life events. Track patterns over time rather than focusing on single assessments."
