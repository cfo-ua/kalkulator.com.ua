  - question: "What's the most water-efficient showerhead?"
    answer: "Efficient showerheads with 6-9 L/min flow rate are most effective. They can reduce water consumption by 40-60% compared to standard showerheads while maintaining comfort through aeration."
  - question: "Is a shower always more economical than a bath?"
    answer: "Not always. A long shower (over 20 minutes) can use more water than a partially filled bathtub. Key factors are shower duration, showerhead type, and bathtub volume."
  - question: "How can I reduce water consumption during showers?"
    answer: "Reduce time to 5-8 minutes, turn off water while soaping, use an efficient showerhead, don't overheat water. These simple steps can reduce consumption by 30-50%."
  - question: "When is it better to take a bath instead of a shower?"
    answer: "Baths can be justified for relaxation, therapeutic procedures, bathing children, or when you need to soak for a long time. In such cases, fill the tub only 1/2-2/3 full."
  - question: "How do I calculate my actual savings from changing habits?"
    answer: "Track your water meter readings for a week before and after changing habits. Multiply the difference by your water rate. Also consider water heating costs (gas/electricity)."
