    <h2>Toothpaste Calculator — Planning Your Healthy Smile</h2>
    <p>
      This <strong>toothpaste calculator</strong> helps calculate the duration of toothpaste tube usage based on individual hygiene habits and product characteristics. Proper planning ensures continuous dental care and helps save on purchases.
    </p>
    
    <h3>How to Use the Toothpaste Calculator</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Enter <strong>tube volume</strong> and <strong>number of people</strong> in household</li>
      <li>Specify <strong>brushing frequency</strong> and <strong>amount of paste per use</strong></li>
      <li>Add information about <strong>toothbrush type</strong> and <strong>brushing technique</strong></li>
      <li>Get predictions for <strong>usage duration</strong> and care recommendations</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Importance of Proper Oral Hygiene</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Cavity Prevention:</strong> Regular brushing removes dental plaque</li>
      <li><strong>Gum Health:</strong> Prevents gingivitis and periodontitis</li>
      <li><strong>Fresh Breath:</strong> Eliminates bacteria causing bad breath</li>
      <li><strong>Overall Health:</strong> Connection between oral health and cardiovascular system</li>
      <li><strong>Aesthetics:</strong> Maintaining whiteness and healthy appearance of teeth</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Toothpaste Consumption Factors</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Tube Size:</strong> From 25ml (children's) to 150ml (family)</li>
      <li><strong>Brushing Frequency:</strong> Recommended 2-3 times daily</li>
      <li><strong>Amount of Paste:</strong> Pea-sized amount (0.5-1g) is optimal</li>
      <li><strong>Brush Type:</strong> Electric brushes require less paste</li>
      <li><strong>Brushing Technique:</strong> Proper technique increases effectiveness</li>
      <li><strong>User Age:</strong> Children use less paste</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Types of Toothpaste</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Fluoride:</strong> Standard for cavity prevention</li>
      <li><strong>Whitening:</strong> With abrasive particles for stain removal</li>
      <li><strong>Sensitive Teeth:</strong> With potassium to reduce pain</li>
      <li><strong>Children's:</strong> Gentle formula with pleasant taste</li>
      <li><strong>Natural:</strong> Without artificial additives</li>
      <li><strong>Therapeutic:</strong> With medicinal components</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Proper Teeth Brushing Technique</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Duration:</strong> Minimum 2 minutes</li>
      <li><strong>Amount of Paste:</strong> Pea-sized amount is sufficient</li>
      <li><strong>Brush Movements:</strong> Gentle circular motions</li>
      <li><strong>Coverage:</strong> All tooth surfaces and gum line</li>
      <li><strong>Rinsing:</strong> Moderate rinsing after brushing</li>
      <li><strong>Brush Replacement:</strong> Every 3-4 months</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Savings and Efficiency Tips</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Don't Overuse:</strong> Large amounts don't improve cleaning</li>
      <li><strong>Store Properly:</strong> Close tube, store in cool place</li>
      <li><strong>Use Completely:</strong> Cut tube to use every last bit</li>
      <li><strong>Choose Quality:</strong> Effective paste = fewer dental problems</li>
      <li><strong>Consult Dentist:</strong> Individual recommendations</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Special Cases</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Braces:</strong> Require more time and paste for cleaning</li>
      <li><strong>Dentures:</strong> Special care products</li>
      <li><strong>Periodontal Problems:</strong> Therapeutic pastes as prescribed</li>
      <li><strong>Pregnancy:</strong> Special attention to gum hygiene</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Economic Aspects</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Prevention Cost:</strong> Much cheaper than treatment</li>
      <li><strong>Family Packages:</strong> More economical than individual tubes</li>
      <li><strong>Sales and Discounts:</strong> Toothpaste has long shelf life</li>
      <li><strong>Quality over Price:</strong> Effective pastes prevent treatment costs</li>
    </ul>

    <p><strong>Important Note:</strong> This calculator provides approximate consumption calculations. Individual needs may vary. For optimal dental care, consult with dentist and hygienist.</p>

  - question: "When should I change toothpaste type?"
