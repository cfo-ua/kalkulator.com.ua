    <h2>Face Shape Analysis for Perfect Sunglasses</h2>
    <p>Our face shape calculator helps you determine your face type and find the perfect sunglasses style. Simply input your facial measurements, and we'll provide personalized recommendations for the most flattering eyewear.</p>
    
    <h3>Main Face Shape Types</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Oval:</strong> Balanced proportions, suits almost all sunglasses styles</li>
      <li><strong>Round:</strong> Width and length nearly equal, needs angular shapes</li>
      <li><strong>Square:</strong> Wide forehead and jaw, suits rounded frames</li>
      <li><strong>Heart:</strong> Wide forehead, narrow chin, suits delicate frames</li>
      <li><strong>Triangle:</strong> Narrow forehead, wide jaw, needs top-heavy styles</li>
      <li><strong>Diamond:</strong> Narrow forehead and chin, wide cheeks, suits oval shapes</li>
    </ul>
    
    <h3>How to Measure Your Face Correctly</h3>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Forehead Width:</strong> Measure from temple to temple at the widest point</li>
      <li><strong>Cheek Width:</strong> Measure under the eyes at the widest part of cheekbones</li>
      <li><strong>Jawline Width:</strong> Measure at the widest part of your jaw</li>
      <li><strong>Face Length:</strong> From center of hairline to tip of chin</li>
    </ol>
    
    <h3>Sunglasses Style Recommendations</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Aviators:</strong> Universal, perfect for oval and heart-shaped faces</li>
      <li><strong>Wayfarers:</strong> Ideal for round and oval faces</li>
      <li><strong>Cat-eye:</strong> Great for square and round faces</li>
      <li><strong>Round frames:</strong> Perfect for square and angular faces</li>
      <li><strong>Rectangular:</strong> Suits round and oval faces</li>
      <li><strong>Wraparound:</strong> Great for active lifestyles, suits most face shapes</li>
    </ul>
    
    <h3>Sunglasses Selection Tips</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Frame should be proportional to your face size</li>
      <li>Frame colors can enhance eye color and skin tone</li>
      <li>Sunglasses width shouldn't exceed face width</li>
      <li>Top frame line should follow your eyebrow line</li>
      <li>Lens center should align with your pupils</li>
      <li>Consider your lifestyle and activities when choosing style</li>
    </ul>
    
    <h3>UV Protection and Lens Quality</h3>
    <p>Beyond style, always ensure your sunglasses provide 100% UV protection (UV400). Quality lenses protect your eyes from harmful UV-A and UV-B rays, preventing eye damage and maintaining long-term eye health.</p>
