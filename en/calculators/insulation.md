    <h2>Insulation Calculator</h2>
    <p>Calculate the exact amount of <strong>insulation needed</strong> for your walls, attic, floors, or ceilings. This professional insulation calculator determines R-value requirements, material quantities, and potential energy savings.</p>

    <h3>How to Calculate Insulation Needs:</h3>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Measure area:</strong> length × width of walls, ceiling, or floor</li>
      <li><strong>Determine R-value:</strong> based on climate zone and building codes</li>
      <li><strong>Choose insulation type:</strong> batts, blown-in, spray foam, or rigid</li>
      <li><strong>Calculate coverage:</strong> account for studs, joists, and obstacles</li>
    </ol>

    <h3>Recommended R-Values by Climate Zone:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Zone 1-2 (South):</strong> R-30 attic, R-13 walls, R-13 floors</li>
      <li><strong>Zone 3-4 (Moderate):</strong> R-38 attic, R-13 walls, R-19 floors</li>
      <li><strong>Zone 5-6 (North):</strong> R-49 attic, R-20 walls, R-25 floors</li>
      <li><strong>Zone 7-8 (Extreme):</strong> R-60 attic, R-21 walls, R-30 floors</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Types of Insulation & R-Values:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Fiberglass batts:</strong> R-3.2 per inch, easy DIY installation</li>
      <li><strong>Blown-in fiberglass:</strong> R-2.5 per inch, fills gaps well</li>
      <li><strong>Cellulose blown-in:</strong> R-3.6 per inch, eco-friendly option</li>
      <li><strong>Spray foam (closed):</strong> R-6.5 per inch, air sealing properties</li>
      <li><strong>Spray foam (open):</strong> R-3.7 per inch, vapor permeable</li>
      <li><strong>Rigid foam boards:</strong> R-4 to R-8 per inch, exterior applications</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Insulation Applications:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Attic floors:</strong> highest priority, greatest energy savings</li>
      <li><strong>Exterior walls:</strong> 2×4 or 2×6 stud cavities</li>
      <li><strong>Basement walls:</strong> foundation insulation, moisture control</li>
      <li><strong>Crawl spaces:</strong> floor insulation or wall insulation</li>
      <li><strong>Cathedral ceilings:</strong> between rafters, continuous air barrier</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Coverage Rates & Packaging:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Batt insulation:</strong> sold by square feet, pre-cut widths</li>
      <li><strong>Blown-in bags:</strong> 25-40 sq ft coverage at R-30</li>
      <li><strong>Spray foam kits:</strong> 15-600 board feet per kit</li>
      <li><strong>Rigid boards:</strong> 4×8 sheets, various thicknesses</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Energy Savings Potential:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Attic insulation:</strong> 10-50% heating/cooling savings</li>
      <li><strong>Wall insulation:</strong> 5-25% energy bill reduction</li>
      <li><strong>Floor insulation:</strong> 5-15% comfort improvement</li>
      <li><strong>Air sealing:</strong> additional 10-20% savings when combined</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Installation Considerations:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Air sealing first:</strong> seal gaps before installing insulation</li>
      <li><strong>Vapor barriers:</strong> face toward heated space in cold climates</li>
      <li><strong>Don't compress:</strong> reduces R-value significantly</li>
      <li><strong>Safety equipment:</strong> masks, gloves, eye protection required</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Cost Estimates (materials only):</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Fiberglass batts:</strong> $0.50-1.50 per sq ft</li>
      <li><strong>Blown-in cellulose:</strong> $1.00-2.00 per sq ft</li>
      <li><strong>Spray foam:</strong> $3.00-7.00 per sq ft</li>
      <li><strong>Rigid foam:</strong> $1.50-4.00 per sq ft</li>
      <li><strong>Professional installation:</strong> double material costs</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Building Code Requirements:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>New construction:</strong> meets current energy codes</li>
      <li><strong>Renovations:</strong> upgrade to current standards when possible</li>
      <li><strong>Permits required:</strong> for major insulation projects</li>
      <li><strong>Inspections:</strong> may be required before drywall installation</li>
    </ul>

  - question: How do I calculate R-value for multiple layers?
    answer: "Add R-values together. Example: R-19 batts + R-30 blown-in = R-49 total. Don't count compressed insulation at full R-value."
