    <h3>Factors That Affect Food Weights</h3>
    
    <h4>Natural Variation</h4>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Size Grades:</strong> Small, medium, large varieties have different weights</li>
      <li><strong>Seasonality:</strong> Fruits and vegetables vary in size throughout the year</li>
      <li><strong>Variety:</strong> Different cultivars of the same food have different average weights</li>
      <li><strong>Growing Conditions:</strong> Weather and soil affect final size and weight</li>
      <li><strong>Ripeness:</strong> Riper fruits may weigh slightly more due to water content</li>
    </ul>

    <h4>Storage & Handling</h4>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Moisture Loss:</strong> Stored produce gradually loses water weight</li>
      <li><strong>Trimming:</strong> Remove stems, leaves, or damaged portions affects weight</li>
      <li><strong>Processing:</strong> Washing and prep can change final usable weight</li>
      <li><strong>Packaging:</strong> Account for any packaging materials in total weight</li>
