    <h2>Gravel Calculator</h2>
    <p>Calculate the exact amount of <strong>gravel needed</strong> for your driveway, pathway, or landscaping project. This professional gravel calculator estimates cubic yards, tons, and costs for all types of gravel and crushed stone.</p>

    <h3>How to Calculate Gravel Requirements:</h3>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Measure your area:</strong> length × width of driveway or path</li>
      <li><strong>Determine depth needed:</strong> based on traffic and base requirements</li>
      <li><strong>Choose gravel type:</strong> affects weight, drainage, and appearance</li>
      <li><strong>Add compaction factor:</strong> 10-15% extra for settling</li>
    </ol>

    <h3>Recommended Gravel Depths:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Driveway base:</strong> 4-6 inches (heavy traffic, cars)</li>
      <li><strong>Walkways:</strong> 2-3 inches (foot traffic only)</li>
      <li><strong>Decorative areas:</strong> 2-3 inches (landscaping, aesthetics)</li>
      <li><strong>Drainage applications:</strong> 6-12 inches (french drains, foundation)</li>
      <li><strong>Parking areas:</strong> 6-8 inches (heavy vehicles, commercial)</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Types of Gravel & Their Uses:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Pea gravel (3/8"):</strong> decorative, walkways, drainage - 1.3 tons/yard</li>
      <li><strong>Crushed stone (3/4"):</strong> driveways, base material - 1.5 tons/yard</li>
      <li><strong>River rock (1-3"):</strong> landscaping, drainage - 1.2 tons/yard</li>
      <li><strong>Decomposed granite:</strong> paths, patio base - 1.4 tons/yard</li>
      <li><strong>Crusher run:</strong> driveway base, compacts well - 1.6 tons/yard</li>
      <li><strong>Marble chips:</strong> decorative, bright white - 1.4 tons/yard</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Gravel Coverage Rates:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>1 cubic yard covers:</strong> 108 sq ft at 3" deep</li>
      <li><strong>1 cubic yard covers:</strong> 81 sq ft at 4" deep</li>
      <li><strong>1 cubic yard covers:</strong> 54 sq ft at 6" deep</li>
      <li><strong>1 ton covers:</strong> 70-100 sq ft depending on gravel type</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Gravel Installation Layers:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Base layer:</strong> crushed stone or crusher run (3-4")</li>
      <li><strong>Middle layer:</strong> smaller crushed stone (1-2")</li>
      <li><strong>Top layer:</strong> finished gravel or decorative stone (1-2")</li>
      <li><strong>Compaction:</strong> between each layer for stability</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Pricing Guide (per ton delivered):</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Basic crushed stone:</strong> $25-35 per ton</li>
      <li><strong>Pea gravel:</strong> $30-45 per ton</li>
      <li><strong>River rock:</strong> $35-55 per ton</li>
      <li><strong>Decorative stone:</strong> $40-80 per ton</li>
      <li><strong>Delivery fee:</strong> $50-150 depending on distance</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Driveway Construction Tips:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Excavate properly:</strong> remove existing material to proper depth</li>
      <li><strong>Install geotextile:</strong> fabric prevents mixing with soil</li>
      <li><strong>Compact each layer:</strong> use plate compactor or roller</li>
      <li><strong>Crown the surface:</strong> 2% slope for drainage</li>
      <li><strong>Edge restraints:</strong> prevent gravel spreading</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Maintenance Requirements:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Regular raking:</strong> redistribute gravel monthly</li>
      <li><strong>Annual top-up:</strong> add 1/2" fresh gravel yearly</li>
      <li><strong>Weed control:</strong> use herbicide or landscape fabric</li>
      <li><strong>Pothole repair:</strong> fill and compact as needed</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Environmental Considerations:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Drainage benefits:</strong> reduces runoff and erosion</li>
      <li><strong>Permeable surface:</strong> allows water infiltration</li>
      <li><strong>Local sourcing:</strong> reduces transportation costs and impact</li>
      <li><strong>Recyclable material:</strong> can be reused in other projects</li>
    </ul>
