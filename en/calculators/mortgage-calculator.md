    <h2>Mortgage Calculator - Calculate Your Monthly Home Payment</h2>
    <p>Calculate your <strong>monthly mortgage payment</strong> including principal, interest, taxes, insurance, and PMI. Get a complete breakdown of your home loan costs and view detailed amortization schedules.</p>

    <h3>What's Included in Your Mortgage Payment (PITI):</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Principal:</strong> The amount that goes toward paying down your loan balance</li>
      <li><strong>Interest:</strong> The cost of borrowing money from the lender</li>
      <li><strong>Property Taxes:</strong> Annual property taxes divided by 12 months</li>
      <li><strong>Insurance:</strong> Homeowners insurance to protect your investment</li>
      <li><strong>PMI:</strong> Private Mortgage Insurance (if down payment is less than 20%)</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Current Mortgage Rates (2023):</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>30-year fixed:</strong> 6.5% - 7.5% (varies by lender and credit)</li>
      <li><strong>15-year fixed:</strong> 6.0% - 7.0% (typically 0.5% lower than 30-year)</li>
      <li><strong>5/1 ARM:</strong> 5.5% - 6.5% (adjustable after 5 years)</li>
      <li><strong>FHA loans:</strong> 6.0% - 7.0% (plus MIP insurance)</li>
      <li><strong>VA loans:</strong> 6.0% - 7.0% (no down payment required)</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Down Payment Options:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>20% or more:</strong> No PMI required, better rates</li>
      <li><strong>10-19%:</strong> PMI required, conventional loans available</li>
      <li><strong>5-9%:</strong> Higher PMI, may need higher credit scores</li>
      <li><strong>3-5%:</strong> First-time buyer programs, FHA loans</li>
      <li><strong>0%:</strong> VA loans (veterans), USDA loans (rural areas)</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Types of Mortgage Loans:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Conventional Loans:</strong> Not government-backed, typically require 5-20% down</li>
      <li><strong>FHA Loans:</strong> Government-backed, 3.5% down payment, lower credit requirements</li>
      <li><strong>VA Loans:</strong> For veterans, 0% down payment, no PMI</li>
      <li><strong>USDA Loans:</strong> For rural properties, 0% down payment</li>
      <li><strong>Jumbo Loans:</strong> For high-value homes exceeding conforming loan limits</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Loan Terms Comparison:</h3>
    <p><strong>30-Year Mortgage:</strong></p>
    <ul>
      <li>Lower monthly payments</li>
      <li>More total interest paid</li>
      <li>Better cash flow for other investments</li>
      <li>Easier qualification due to lower payment</li>
    </ul>

    <p><strong>15-Year Mortgage:</strong></p>
    <ul>
      <li>Higher monthly payments</li>
      <li>Significantly less total interest</li>
      <li>Build equity faster</li>
      <li>Typically lower interest rates</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI):</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>When required:</strong> Down payment less than 20%</li>
      <li><strong>Cost:</strong> 0.3% to 1.5% of loan amount annually</li>
      <li><strong>Removal:</strong> When you reach 20% equity (or 22% automatically)</li>
      <li><strong>Types:</strong> Monthly PMI, upfront PMI, lender-paid PMI</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Property Taxes:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>National average:</strong> 1.0% - 1.5% of home value annually</li>
      <li><strong>High-tax states:</strong> New Jersey (2.4%), Illinois (2.3%), New Hampshire (2.2%)</li>
      <li><strong>Low-tax states:</strong> Hawaii (0.3%), Alabama (0.4%), Louisiana (0.6%)</li>
      <li><strong>Escrow:</strong> Usually collected monthly with mortgage payment</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Homeowners Insurance:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>National average:</strong> $1,200 - $2,000 annually</li>
      <li><strong>Factors affecting cost:</strong> Location, home value, deductible, coverage</li>
      <li><strong>Required by lender:</strong> Protects both you and the bank</li>
      <li><strong>Additional coverage:</strong> Flood insurance (separate policy if needed)</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>How to Lower Your Mortgage Payment:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Increase down payment:</strong> Reduce loan amount and eliminate PMI</li>
      <li><strong>Improve credit score:</strong> Get better interest rates</li>
      <li><strong>Shop around:</strong> Compare rates from multiple lenders</li>
      <li><strong>Consider points:</strong> Pay upfront to reduce interest rate</li>
      <li><strong>Choose longer term:</strong> 30-year vs 15-year (but more total interest)</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Refinancing Considerations:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Rate improvement:</strong> Generally worthwhile if rates drop 0.5-1%</li>
      <li><strong>Break-even analysis:</strong> Calculate how long to recoup closing costs</li>
      <li><strong>Cash-out refinance:</strong> Access home equity for other needs</li>
      <li><strong>Term changes:</strong> Switch from 30-year to 15-year or vice versa</li>
    </ul>

    <p>Use this calculator to explore different scenarios and find the mortgage payment that fits your budget. Remember to factor in all costs of homeownership including maintenance, utilities, and potential HOA fees.</p>
