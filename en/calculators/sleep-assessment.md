    <h2>Sleep Quality Assessment Calculator - Comprehensive Sleep Health Evaluation</h2>
    <p>
      This comprehensive <strong>sleep quality assessment calculator</strong> evaluates your sleep health based on multiple factors including sleep duration, age, gender, lifestyle habits, and sleep patterns. Get a detailed analysis of your sleep quality with personalized recommendations for better sleep hygiene and overall health.
    </p>
    
    <h3>How to Use This Sleep Assessment Tool</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Enter your <strong>age, gender, and typical sleep duration</strong></li>
      <li>Answer questions about your <strong>sleep patterns and habits</strong></li>
      <li>Provide information about <strong>lifestyle factors</strong> affecting sleep</li>
      <li>Receive a comprehensive <strong>sleep quality score</strong> with detailed analysis</li>
      <li>Get personalized <strong>recommendations</strong> for improving sleep quality</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Why Sleep Quality Assessment Matters</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Health Impact:</strong> Poor sleep linked to heart disease, diabetes, obesity, and depression</li>
      <li><strong>Cognitive Function:</strong> Sleep affects memory, concentration, and decision-making</li>
      <li><strong>Immune System:</strong> Quality sleep strengthens immune response and recovery</li>
      <li><strong>Mental Health:</strong> Adequate sleep crucial for emotional regulation and stress management</li>
      <li><strong>Longevity:</strong> Good sleep habits associated with increased life expectancy</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Scientific Assessment Criteria</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Sleep Duration:</strong> Age-appropriate sleep length recommendations from sleep medicine</li>
      <li><strong>Sleep Efficiency:</strong> Time spent sleeping vs. time in bed</li>
      <li><strong>Sleep Onset:</strong> Time taken to fall asleep (sleep latency)</li>
      <li><strong>Sleep Continuity:</strong> Number and duration of nighttime awakenings</li>
      <li><strong>Circadian Rhythm:</strong> Consistency of sleep-wake patterns</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Understanding Your Sleep Score</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Excellent (90-100):</strong> Optimal sleep quality with minimal areas for improvement</li>
      <li><strong>Good (75-89):</strong> Generally healthy sleep with minor issues to address</li>
      <li><strong>Fair (60-74):</strong> Moderate sleep quality with several areas for improvement</li>
      <li><strong>Poor (40-59):</strong> Significant sleep issues requiring attention</li>
      <li><strong>Very Poor (<40):</strong> Severe sleep problems - consider professional help</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Factors Affecting Sleep Quality</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Age:</strong> Sleep needs and patterns change throughout life</li>
      <li><strong>Lifestyle:</strong> Exercise, diet, caffeine, alcohol consumption</li>
      <li><strong>Environment:</strong> Room temperature, noise, light exposure</li>
      <li><strong>Stress:</strong> Work pressure, life events, anxiety levels</li>
      <li><strong>Health Conditions:</strong> Sleep disorders, chronic illness, medications</li>
      <li><strong>Sleep Hygiene:</strong> Bedtime routine, screen time, bedroom setup</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Recommended Sleep Duration by Age</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>18-25 years:</strong> 7-9 hours (optimal: 8 hours)</li>
      <li><strong>26-64 years:</strong> 7-9 hours (optimal: 7-8 hours)</li>
      <li><strong>65+ years:</strong> 7-8 hours (optimal: 7-8 hours)</li>
      <li><strong>Individual Variation:</strong> Some people function well on slightly less or more</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Sleep Improvement Strategies</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Consistent Schedule:</strong> Go to bed and wake up at the same time daily</li>
      <li><strong>Sleep Environment:</strong> Cool (60-67°F), dark, quiet bedroom</li>
      <li><strong>Pre-Sleep Routine:</strong> Relaxing activities 30-60 minutes before bed</li>
      <li><strong>Limit Stimulants:</strong> Avoid caffeine 6+ hours before bedtime</li>
      <li><strong>Exercise Regularly:</strong> But not within 3 hours of bedtime</li>
      <li><strong>Manage Stress:</strong> Meditation, journaling, relaxation techniques</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>When to Seek Professional Help</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Chronic insomnia lasting more than 3 weeks</li>
      <li>Loud snoring with breathing interruptions</li>
      <li>Excessive daytime sleepiness despite adequate sleep</li>
      <li>Restless legs or periodic limb movements</li>
      <li>Sleep-related anxiety or depression</li>
      <li>Shift work sleep problems</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Benefits of Quality Sleep</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Physical Health:</strong> Improved immune function, better metabolism</li>
      <li><strong>Mental Performance:</strong> Enhanced memory, creativity, problem-solving</li>
      <li><strong>Emotional Wellbeing:</strong> Better mood regulation, reduced anxiety</li>
      <li><strong>Athletic Performance:</strong> Faster recovery, improved coordination</li>
      <li><strong>Appearance:</strong> Healthier skin, reduced signs of aging</li>
    </ul>

    <p><strong>Important:</strong> This assessment provides general guidance based on sleep research and clinical guidelines. It's not a substitute for professional medical diagnosis. If you have persistent sleep problems or suspect a sleep disorder, consult a healthcare provider or sleep specialist.</p>
