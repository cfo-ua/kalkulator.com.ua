    <h2>How does the AI Prompt Cost Calculator work?</h2>
    <p>This calculator helps estimate costs for using popular AI APIs for text generation, chatbots, and other applications. Calculations are based on token counts and current provider pricing rates.</p>
    
    <h3>Understanding tokens in AI</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><b>Token</b> — basic unit of text for AI models (word, word part, or character)</li>
      <li><b>Input tokens</b> — your prompt and context sent to the AI</li>
      <li><b>Output tokens</b> — response generated by the AI model</li>
      <li><b>Approximately</b> — 1 token ≈ 0.75 words in English, 1 word ≈ 1.3 tokens</li>
    </ul>
    
    <h3>Popular AI API providers</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><b>OpenAI</b> — GPT-3.5, GPT-4, GPT-4 Turbo models</li>
      <li><b>Anthropic</b> — Claude 3 Sonnet, Haiku, Opus</li>
      <li><b>Google</b> — PaLM 2, Gemini Pro models</li>
      <li><b>Cohere</b> — Command models for business applications</li>
    </ul>
    
    <h3>Typical use cases and costs</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><b>Chatbots</b> — typically $10-100/month depending on traffic</li>
      <li><b>Content generation</b> — $50-500/month for regular use</li>
      <li><b>Text analysis</b> — $20-200/month for document processing</li>
      <li><b>Code assistance</b> — $30-300/month for developers</li>
    </ul>
    
    <h3>Cost optimization tips</h3>

      <li>Set token limits to control costs</li>
      <li>Use batch processing when possible</li>
      <li>Consider open-source alternatives for high volume</li>

    answer: |
      Tokens are pieces of words that AI uses to process text. On average, 1 word = 1.3 tokens. For example, "Hello, how are you?" contains about 4 tokens. Pricing depends on both input and output token counts.
  - question: "How much does ChatGPT API cost?"
    answer: |
      As of 2024: GPT-3.5 Turbo costs $0.001-0.002 per 1K tokens, GPT-4 costs $0.01-0.06 per 1K tokens depending on version. For typical chatbots, this translates to $10-100/month with moderate usage.
  - question: "What's the difference between GPT-3.5 and GPT-4?"
    answer: |
      GPT-4 is significantly smarter and more accurate but costs 10-30x more than GPT-3.5. GPT-3.5 is suitable for simple tasks, while GPT-4 excels at complex analytical and creative tasks.
  - question: "How to optimize AI API costs?"
    answer: |
      Use smaller models for simple tasks, shorten prompts, cache responses, set token limits, use batch processing, consider self-hosted solutions for high volumes, and implement smart prompt engineering.
  - question: "What are rate limits in AI APIs?"
    answer: |
      Rate limits restrict the number of requests per minute/hour. New users have lower limits that increase over time. Exceeding limits results in blocked or throttled requests.
  - question: "Is it safe to send confidential data to AI APIs?"
    answer: |
      Most providers claim they don't store user data, but for sensitive information, consider local models or enterprise plans with additional security guarantees and data processing agreements.
  - question: "How to compare quality of different AI models?"
    answer: |
      Test on your specific tasks. GPT-4 excels at complex tasks, Claude 3 is great for analysis, GPT-3.5 offers optimal price/performance for simple tasks. Consider accuracy, creativity, and reasoning needs.
  - question: "What to do if AI costs are too high?"
    answer: |
      Analyze usage patterns, switch to cheaper models for simple tasks, implement caching, optimize prompts, use streaming for UX without cost increase, consider open-source alternatives for volume.
