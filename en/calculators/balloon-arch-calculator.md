    <h2>Perfect Balloon Arch Planning</h2>
    <p>Our balloon arch calculator helps you determine the exact number of balloons needed to create a stunning balloon arch of any size. We consider arch type, balloon sizes, and decoration style for accurate results.</p>
    
    <h3>Types of Balloon Arches</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Classic Arch:</strong> Evenly spaced balloons of uniform size</li>
      <li><strong>Organic Arch:</strong> Mixed balloon sizes for natural appearance</li>
      <li><strong>Spiral Arch:</strong> Balloons arranged in spiral pattern with 2-3 colors</li>
      <li><strong>Gradient Arch:</strong> Smooth color transitions</li>
      <li><strong>Themed Arch:</strong> With additional decorative elements</li>
    </ul>
    
    <h3>Balloon Sizes and Their Uses</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>5" (12 cm):</strong> Small balloons for filling gaps</li>
      <li><strong>9" (23 cm):</strong> Medium balloons, base for most arches</li>
      <li><strong>11" (28 cm):</strong> Large balloons for main structure</li>
      <li><strong>12" (30 cm):</strong> Standard large balloons</li>
      <li><strong>16" (40 cm):</strong> Extra large for accents</li>
      <li><strong>18" (45 cm):</strong> Giant balloons for focal points</li>
    </ul>
    
    <h3>Arch Density Styles</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Dense Arch:</strong> Maximum balloon count, full coverage</li>
      <li><strong>Medium Density:</strong> Balanced look with some gaps</li>
      <li><strong>Light Arch:</strong> Minimal balloons, airy appearance</li>
      <li><strong>Asymmetric:</strong> Uneven distribution for modern look</li>
    </ul>
    
    <h3>Arch Creation Tips</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Always buy 10-15% extra balloons for backup</li>
      <li>Use quality latex balloons for durability</li>
      <li>Mix different sizes for more natural appearance</li>
      <li>Consider lighting when choosing colors</li>
      <li>Prepare arch day before event for best results</li>
    </ul>
    
    <h3>Materials and Tools</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Balloons:</strong> Latex balloons in various sizes and colors</li>
      <li><strong>Frame:</strong> Wire base, PVC pipes, or fishing line</li>
      <li><strong>Fasteners:</strong> Zip ties, clips, adhesive tape</li>
      <li><strong>Tools:</strong> Balloon pump (essential!)</li>
      <li><strong>Additional:</strong> Scissors, marker, measuring tape</li>
    </ul>
    
    <h3>Color Scheme Recommendations</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Monochrome:</strong> Shades of one color for elegance</li>
      <li><strong>Complementary:</strong> Opposite colors for contrast</li>
      <li><strong>Analogous:</strong> Adjacent colors for harmony</li>
      <li><strong>Themed:</strong> Colors matching event theme</li>
      <li><strong>Metallic:</strong> Gold, silver, rose gold for premium look</li>
    </ul>
    
    <h3>Balloon Arch Maintenance</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Air-filled balloons last 3-5 days indoors</li>
      <li>Helium balloons last 12-24 hours only</li>
      <li>Avoid direct sunlight and heat sources</li>
      <li>Keep away from sharp objects and rough surfaces</li>
      <li>Use Hi-Float treatment for longer helium balloon life</li>
    </ul>

  - question: How long does a balloon arch last?
    answer: "Quality latex balloons filled with air last 3-5 days. Helium balloons last 12-24 hours. For longer-lasting arches, use air-filled balloons."
  - question: What balloon colors are best for weddings?
    answer: "Popular wedding colors include: white with rose gold, pastels, white with greenery, cream tones. Avoid overly bright colors for elegant weddings."
  - question: Do I need a special balloon pump?
    answer: "Yes, a hand or electric balloon pump significantly speeds up the process and ensures uniform balloon size. Inflating by mouth is inefficient and tiring."
  - question: How much does a balloon arch cost?
    answer: "Cost depends on balloon quantity, quality, and complexity. Average: $2-6 per linear foot for simple arch, $8-20 per foot for complex designs."
  - question: What mistakes do beginners commonly make?
    answer: "Common mistakes: underestimating balloon count, using cheap balloons, wrong sizing, no backup balloons, inflating too early before event."
  - question: Can I mix helium and air balloons in an arch?
    answer: "Yes, but consider that helium balloons will pull upward. Better to use helium for accents and air-filled balloons for the main structure."
  - question: How early should I start making the arch?
    answer: "For air-filled arches, start 1-2 days before the event. For helium arches, make them on the day of the event for best appearance."
  - question: What's the difference between organic and classic arches?
    answer: "Classic arches use uniform balloon sizes in regular patterns. Organic arches use mixed sizes and irregular spacing for a more natural, flowing appearance."
