    <h2>Hair Growth Calculator — Scientific Approach to Hairstyle Planning</h2>
    <p>
      This <strong>hair growth calculator</strong> helps predict hair growth rate and future length based on individual characteristics and external factors. Understanding natural hair growth processes allows effective hairstyle planning and care optimization.
    </p>
    
    <h3>How to Use the Hair Growth Calculator</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Enter your <strong>current hair length</strong> and <strong>desired length</strong></li>
      <li>Specify <strong>personal characteristics</strong> (age, gender, hair type)</li>
      <li>Add information about <strong>lifestyle and care routine</strong></li>
      <li>Get predictions for <strong>time to reach desired length</strong> and recommendations</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Hair Growth Science</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Anagen Phase:</strong> Active growth lasting 2-7 years</li>
      <li><strong>Catagen Phase:</strong> Transition phase 2-3 weeks</li>
      <li><strong>Telogen Phase:</strong> Resting phase 2-3 months</li>
      <li><strong>Average Rate:</strong> 1-1.5 cm per month</li>
      <li><strong>Genetic Program:</strong> Determines maximum length</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Factors Affecting Hair Growth</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Genetics:</strong> Primary factor for rate and length</li>
      <li><strong>Age:</strong> Rate decreases with age</li>
      <li><strong>Hormones:</strong> Estrogen stimulates, androgens may inhibit</li>
      <li><strong>Nutrition:</strong> Proteins, iron, B vitamins are critical</li>
      <li><strong>Health:</strong> Diseases can slow growth</li>
      <li><strong>Stress:</strong> Chronic stress negatively impacts growth</li>
      <li><strong>Season:</strong> Hair grows faster in summer</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Nutrition for Healthy Hair Growth</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Proteins:</strong> Meat, fish, eggs, legumes - building blocks</li>
      <li><strong>Iron:</strong> Red meat, spinach - oxygen transport</li>
      <li><strong>Zinc:</strong> Nuts, seeds - protein synthesis</li>
      <li><strong>Vitamin D:</strong> Fish, eggs - follicle activation</li>
      <li><strong>Biotin:</strong> Eggs, avocado - keratin health</li>
      <li><strong>Omega-3:</strong> Fish, flax - moisture and shine</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Care for Growth Stimulation</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Gentle Massage:</strong> Stimulates scalp circulation</li>
      <li><strong>Proper Washing:</strong> With warm (not hot) water</li>
      <li><strong>Sun Protection:</strong> UV filters for hair</li>
      <li><strong>Heat Limitation:</strong> Minimize blow dryers and irons</li>
      <li><strong>Soft Hair Ties:</strong> Without metal elements</li>
      <li><strong>Regular Trimming:</strong> Remove split ends</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Myths and Reality</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Myth:</strong> Frequent cutting speeds growth - actually has no effect</li>
      <li><strong>Myth:</strong> 100 brush strokes - can damage hair</li>
      <li><strong>Reality:</strong> Growth depends on follicle health</li>
      <li><strong>Reality:</strong> Stress can halt growth for months</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Special Cases</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>After Chemotherapy:</strong> Slower start, then acceleration</li>
      <li><strong>Postpartum Period:</strong> Temporary shedding, then recovery</li>
      <li><strong>Menopause:</strong> Slowing due to hormonal changes</li>
      <li><strong>Diets:</strong> Rapid weight loss can slow growth</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>When to See a Specialist</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Sudden Growth Slowdown:</strong> Less than 0.5 cm per month</li>
      <li><strong>Excessive Shedding:</strong> More than 100 hairs per day</li>
      <li><strong>Bald Patches:</strong> Possible autoimmune disorders</li>
      <li><strong>Texture Changes:</strong> Sudden changes may signal problems</li>
    </ul>

    <p><strong>Important Note:</strong> This calculator provides approximate predictions based on average statistical data. Individual growth rates can vary significantly. For hair health problems, consultation with a trichologist is recommended.</p>

faq:
  - question: "What is the normal hair growth rate?"
    answer: "Normal hair growth rate is 1-1.5 cm per month, or approximately 12-18 cm per year. In young people, hair can grow up to 2 cm per month, with rate decreasing with age."
  - question: "How long does it take to grow hair to shoulder length?"
    answer: "To grow hair from a short cut (5 cm) to shoulder length (25 cm) typically takes 15-20 months at an average growth rate of 1.2 cm per month."
  - question: "Can you speed up hair growth?"
    answer: "Natural growth rate cannot be dramatically changed, but conditions can be optimized: balanced nutrition, scalp massage, stress reduction, proper care. This can increase rate by 10-20%."
  - question: "Why has my hair stopped growing?"
    answer: "Causes include: stress, hormonal changes, nutrient deficiency, illness, medications, chemical treatments. For prolonged growth cessation, consult a trichologist."
  - question: "Does cutting affect growth rate?"
    answer: "No, trimming ends doesn't affect growth rate from roots. However, regular trimming of split ends helps preserve length by preventing breakage."
  - question: "What vitamins are best for hair growth?"
    answer: "Key vitamins: biotin (B7), B12, D, iron, zinc, omega-3. Better to get from food: eggs, fish, nuts, green vegetables. Take supplements only as prescribed by a doctor."
  - question: "Does hair grow faster in summer?"
    answer: "Yes, hair grows 10-15% faster in summer due to better blood circulation, more sunlight, and vitamin D. Growth slows in winter due to cold and stress."
