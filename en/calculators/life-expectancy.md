Get personalized insights into factors that influence longevity and actionable recommendations for healthy aging.
    </p>

    <h3>Scientific Factors Influencing Life Expectancy</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Genetics (25%):</strong> Family history, inherited health conditions, longevity genes</li>
      <li><strong>Lifestyle (60%):</strong> Diet, exercise, smoking, alcohol, stress management</li>
      <li><strong>Environment (10%):</strong> Air quality, living location, occupational hazards</li>
      <li><strong>Healthcare (5%):</strong> Access to medical care, preventive screenings, health monitoring</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Key Longevity Factors Assessed</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Diet Quality:</strong> Mediterranean diet, processed food intake, nutrition patterns</li>
      <li><strong>Physical Activity:</strong> Regular exercise, cardio fitness, strength training</li>
      <li><strong>Sleep Quality:</strong> Sleep duration, sleep hygiene, circadian rhythm health</li>
      <li><strong>Stress Management:</strong> Chronic stress levels, coping mechanisms, mental health</li>
      <li><strong>Social Connections:</strong> Relationships, community involvement, social support</li>
      <li><strong>Substance Use:</strong> Smoking, alcohol consumption, drug use history</li>
      <li><strong>Mental Health:</strong> Depression, anxiety, cognitive function, life satisfaction</li>
      <li><strong>Preventive Care:</strong> Regular checkups, screenings, vaccinations</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Understanding Life Expectancy Estimates</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Statistical Prediction:</strong> Based on current health research and demographic data</li>
      <li><strong>Individual Variation:</strong> Actual lifespan can vary significantly from estimates</li>
      <li><strong>Modifiable Factors:</strong> Many factors can be changed to improve longevity</li>
      <li><strong>Continuous Updates:</strong> Estimates change as lifestyle and health factors evolve</li>
      <li><strong>Quality vs. Quantity:</strong> Focus on both lifespan and healthspan (healthy years)</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Evidence-Based Longevity Strategies</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Blue Zone Principles:</strong> Lessons from world's longest-living populations</li>
      <li><strong>Caloric Restriction:</strong> Moderate calorie reduction with optimal nutrition</li>
      <li><strong>Intermittent Fasting:</strong> Time-restricted eating patterns for cellular health</li>
      <li><strong>Regular Exercise:</strong> Combination of cardio, strength, and flexibility training</li>
      <li><strong>Stress Reduction:</strong> Meditation, yoga, social support, purposeful living</li>
      <li><strong>Sleep Optimization:</strong> 7-9 hours of quality sleep for cellular repair</li>
      <li><strong>Social Engagement:</strong> Strong relationships and community connections</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Global Life Expectancy Trends</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Developed Countries:</strong> Average 80-85 years (Japan, Switzerland, Australia)</li>
      <li><strong>Developing Countries:</strong> Average 65-75 years (improving rapidly)</li>
      <li><strong>Gender Differences:</strong> Women typically live 4-6 years longer than men</li>
      <li><strong>Increasing Trends:</strong> Global life expectancy increases ~3 months per year</li>
      <li><strong>Health Disparities:</strong> Socioeconomic factors significantly impact longevity</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Lifestyle Modifications for Longevity</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Nutrition:</strong> Plant-rich diet, omega-3 fatty acids, antioxidants, limited processed foods</li>
      <li><strong>Exercise:</strong> 150+ minutes moderate activity weekly, strength training 2x/week</li>
      <li><strong>Sleep:</strong> Consistent sleep schedule, 7-9 hours nightly, sleep hygiene</li>
      <li><strong>Stress:</strong> Mindfulness, meditation, hobbies, work-life balance</li>
      <li><strong>Relationships:</strong> Maintain close friendships, family connections, community involvement</li>
      <li><strong>Purpose:</strong> Meaningful work, volunteering, personal growth, lifelong learning</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Environmental Factors Affecting Longevity</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Air Quality:</strong> Pollution exposure, indoor air quality, proximity to traffic</li>
      <li><strong>Water Quality:</strong> Clean drinking water, contaminant exposure</li>
      <li><strong>Climate:</strong> Temperature extremes, natural disaster frequency</li>
      <li><strong>Safety:</strong> Crime rates, accident risks, occupational hazards</li>
      <li><strong>Healthcare Access:</strong> Medical facilities, preventive care availability</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Age-Related Health Considerations</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>20s-30s:</strong> Establish healthy habits, manage stress, build relationships</li>
      <li><strong>40s-50s:</strong> Prevent chronic disease, maintain fitness, career balance</li>
      <li><strong>60s-70s:</strong> Stay active, manage health conditions, social engagement</li>
      <li><strong>80s+:</strong> Maintain independence, cognitive health, quality of life</li>
    </ul>

    <p><strong>Important Disclaimer:</strong> This calculator provides estimates based on statistical data and research. Individual life expectancy can vary significantly due to unforeseen circumstances, medical advances, lifestyle changes, and genetic factors. This tool is for educational purposes and should not replace professional medical advice.</p>
