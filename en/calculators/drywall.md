    <h2>Drywall Calculator</h2>
    <p>Calculate the exact amount of <strong>drywall materials</strong> needed for your construction or renovation project. This professional drywall calculator estimates sheets, joint compound, tape, screws, and finishing supplies.</p>

    <h3>How to Calculate Drywall Needs:</h3>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Measure wall area:</strong> length × height for each wall</li>
      <li><strong>Add ceiling area</strong> if installing ceiling drywall</li>
      <li><strong>Subtract openings:</strong> doors, windows, large fixtures</li>
      <li><strong>Choose sheet size:</strong> 4×8, 4×10, or 4×12 sheets</li>
    </ol>

    <h3>Standard Drywall Sheet Sizes:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>4×8 sheets:</strong> 32 sq ft, most common residential size</li>
      <li><strong>4×10 sheets:</strong> 40 sq ft, reduces seams for 9-10' ceilings</li>
      <li><strong>4×12 sheets:</strong> 48 sq ft, best for high ceilings, fewer seams</li>
      <li><strong>4×16 sheets:</strong> 64 sq ft, commercial applications</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Drywall Thickness Applications:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>1/4 inch:</strong> curved walls, repair work, overlay</li>
      <li><strong>3/8 inch:</strong> walls over existing drywall</li>
      <li><strong>1/2 inch:</strong> standard residential walls and ceilings</li>
      <li><strong>5/8 inch:</strong> fire-rated applications, commercial use</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Specialty Drywall Types:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Regular drywall:</strong> standard interior applications</li>
      <li><strong>Moisture-resistant:</strong> bathrooms, kitchens (green board)</li>
      <li><strong>Mold-resistant:</strong> high-humidity areas (purple board)</li>
      <li><strong>Fire-resistant:</strong> type X, commercial and safety applications</li>
      <li><strong>Soundproof:</strong> extra-thick for noise reduction</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Joint Compound Requirements:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Taping coat:</strong> 0.5 gallons per 1000 sq ft</li>
      <li><strong>Fill coat:</strong> 1.0 gallons per 1000 sq ft</li>
      <li><strong>Finish coat:</strong> 1.5 gallons per 1000 sq ft</li>
      <li><strong>Total compound:</strong> ~3 gallons per 1000 sq ft</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Fastener Requirements:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Drywall screws:</strong> 5 lbs per 1000 sq ft (walls)</li>
      <li><strong>Ceiling screws:</strong> 7 lbs per 1000 sq ft (closer spacing)</li>
      <li><strong>Screw spacing:</strong> 12" on center walls, 8" on center ceilings</li>
      <li><strong>Screw length:</strong> 1-1/4" for 1/2" drywall, 1-5/8" for 5/8"</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Cost Estimates (per sheet/material):</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>1/2" drywall:</strong> $12-18 per 4×8 sheet</li>
      <li><strong>5/8" drywall:</strong> $15-22 per 4×8 sheet</li>
      <li><strong>Moisture-resistant:</strong> $16-24 per 4×8 sheet</li>
      <li><strong>Joint compound:</strong> $15-25 per 5-gallon bucket</li>
      <li><strong>Drywall tape:</strong> $8-12 per 500-foot roll</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Installation Planning:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Deliver materials:</strong> coordinate timing with framing completion</li>
      <li><strong>Storage:</strong> keep drywall flat and dry</li>
      <li><strong>Installation order:</strong> ceilings first, then walls</li>
      <li><strong>Finishing schedule:</strong> 3 coats with drying time between</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Professional Tips:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Order 10% extra:</strong> for cuts, waste, and repairs</li>
      <li><strong>Plan sheet layout:</strong> minimize seams and waste</li>
      <li><strong>Use longer sheets:</strong> reduces horizontal seams</li>
      <li><strong>Quality tools:</strong> good knife, T-square, and screw gun</li>
    </ul>
