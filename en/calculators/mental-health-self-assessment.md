    <h2>Mental Health Self-Assessment Calculator - Comprehensive Psychological Wellbeing Evaluation</h2>
    <p>
      This comprehensive <strong>mental health self-assessment calculator</strong> provides a thorough evaluation of your psychological wellbeing across multiple domains including mood, anxiety, stress, sleep, social functioning, and cognitive health. Get personalized insights and evidence-based recommendations to support your mental wellness journey.
    </p>
    
    <h3>How to Use This Mental Health Assessment Tool</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Answer all questions <strong>honestly and thoughtfully</strong> based on your recent experiences</li>
      <li>Consider your feelings and behaviors over the <strong>past two weeks</strong></li>
      <li>Select the response that <strong>best describes your typical experience</strong></li>
      <li>Receive a comprehensive <strong>mental health score</strong> with detailed analysis</li>
      <li>Get personalized <strong>recommendations</strong> for mental health improvement</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Assessment Domains Covered</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Mood & Emotional Regulation:</strong> Depression symptoms, emotional stability, mood swings</li>
      <li><strong>Anxiety & Worry:</strong> Generalized anxiety, panic symptoms, social anxiety</li>
      <li><strong>Stress Management:</strong> Stress tolerance, coping mechanisms, overwhelm</li>
      <li><strong>Sleep & Energy:</strong> Sleep quality, fatigue levels, energy patterns</li>
      <li><strong>Social Functioning:</strong> Relationships, social interaction, isolation</li>
      <li><strong>Cognitive Function:</strong> Concentration, memory, decision-making</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Understanding Your Mental Health Score</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Excellent (85-100):</strong> Optimal mental health with strong coping skills</li>
      <li><strong>Good (70-84):</strong> Generally positive mental health with minor concerns</li>
      <li><strong>Moderate (55-69):</strong> Some mental health challenges requiring attention</li>
      <li><strong>Concerning (40-54):</strong> Significant mental health issues needing support</li>
      <li><strong>Severe (<40):</strong> Serious mental health concerns - seek professional help</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Evidence-Based Assessment Criteria</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>PHQ-9 Elements:</strong> Depression screening based on clinical guidelines</li>
      <li><strong>GAD-7 Components:</strong> Anxiety assessment using validated measures</li>
      <li><strong>PSS Elements:</strong> Perceived stress scale components</li>
      <li><strong>WHO-5 Factors:</strong> World Health Organization wellbeing index elements</li>
      <li><strong>Sleep Quality:</strong> Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index components</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Mental Health Warning Signs</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Persistent sadness, hopelessness, or emptiness lasting weeks</li>
      <li>Excessive worry, fear, or anxiety interfering with daily life</li>
      <li>Significant changes in appetite, weight, or sleep patterns</li>
      <li>Loss of interest in activities once enjoyed</li>
      <li>Difficulty concentrating, remembering, or making decisions</li>
      <li>Thoughts of death, suicide, or self-harm</li>
      <li>Substance use to cope with emotions</li>
      <li>Social withdrawal or isolation from others</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Mental Wellness Strategies</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Regular Exercise:</strong> 30 minutes of moderate activity 5 times per week</li>
      <li><strong>Mindfulness Practice:</strong> Daily meditation, deep breathing, or yoga</li>
      <li><strong>Social Connection:</strong> Maintain meaningful relationships and support networks</li>
      <li><strong>Sleep Hygiene:</strong> Consistent sleep schedule, 7-9 hours nightly</li>
      <li><strong>Nutrition:</strong> Balanced diet rich in omega-3s, whole grains, fruits, vegetables</li>
      <li><strong>Stress Management:</strong> Time management, relaxation techniques, boundaries</li>
      <li><strong>Professional Support:</strong> Therapy, counseling, or psychiatric care when needed</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>When to Seek Professional Help</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Symptoms persist for more than two weeks</li>
      <li>Mental health issues interfere with work, relationships, or daily activities</li>
      <li>Thoughts of self-harm or suicide</li>
      <li>Substance use as a coping mechanism</li>
      <li>Family history of mental health conditions</li>
      <li>Previous mental health episodes</li>
      <li>Significant life stressors or trauma</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Mental Health Resources</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Crisis Support:</strong> National Suicide Prevention Lifeline: 988</li>
      <li><strong>Text Support:</strong> Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741</li>
      <li><strong>Professional Help:</strong> Therapists, psychiatrists, mental health counselors</li>
      <li><strong>Support Groups:</strong> NAMI, Depression and Bipolar Support Alliance</li>
      <li><strong>Online Resources:</strong> Mental Health America, NIMH, Psychology Today</li>
    </ul>

    <p><strong>Important Disclaimer:</strong> This assessment is for educational and informational purposes only. It is not a substitute for professional mental health diagnosis or treatment. If you are experiencing mental health concerns, especially thoughts of self-harm, please consult with a qualified mental health professional immediately.</p>
