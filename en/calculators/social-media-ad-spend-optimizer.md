  - question: "How do I calculate ROAS for social media advertising?"
    answer: "ROAS = Revenue Generated ÷ Ad Spend. For example, if you spend $1,000 on ads and generate $4,000 in revenue, your ROAS is 4:1. Track revenue from each platform separately for accurate comparison."
  - question: "What's a good ROAS for social media ads?"
    answer: "Varies by industry: E-commerce typically needs 4:1 minimum, SaaS 3:1+, lead generation 2-3:1. Consider customer lifetime value, profit margins, and business goals when setting ROAS targets."
  - question: "How should I allocate budget across different social platforms?"
    answer: "Start with 60% on proven performers, 30% on secondary platforms, 10% for testing new channels. Adjust based on performance data, audience overlap, and campaign objectives."
  - question: "What metrics should I track for social media ad optimization?"
    answer: "Key metrics: ROAS, CAC, conversion rate, CTR, CPC, frequency, reach, and lifetime value. Track these consistently across platforms for accurate performance comparison."
  - question: "How often should I optimize my social media ad spend?"
    answer: "Review daily, optimize weekly, and make major budget adjustments monthly. Real-time monitoring helps catch issues early, while longer periods provide statistically significant data."
  - question: "Should I focus on one social platform or diversify?"
    answer: "Diversify to reduce risk and reach different audiences, but don't spread too thin. Master 2-3 platforms that perform well for your business before expanding to others."
  - question: "How do I handle seasonal fluctuations in ad performance?"
    answer: "Analyze historical data to identify patterns, increase budgets during peak seasons, adjust targeting for seasonal keywords, and prepare different creative assets for various seasons."
  - question: "What's the relationship between ad frequency and performance?"
    answer: "Frequency 1-3 is typically optimal. Higher frequency can cause ad fatigue and decreased performance. Monitor frequency and refresh creatives when performance declines."
