    <h2>Mortgage Affordability Calculator - Determine Your Home Buying Budget</h2>
    <p>Calculate <strong>how much house you can afford</strong> based on your income, monthly debts, down payment, and other factors. Get realistic estimates for your home buying budget and monthly mortgage payments.</p>

    <h3>Key Affordability Rules:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>28% Rule:</strong> Housing costs should not exceed 28% of gross monthly income</li>
      <li><strong>36% Rule:</strong> Total debt payments should not exceed 36% of gross monthly income</li>
      <li><strong>Debt-to-Income Ratio:</strong> Lenders typically require DTI below 43-50%</li>
      <li><strong>Front-End Ratio:</strong> PITI (Principal, Interest, Taxes, Insurance) vs. income</li>
      <li><strong>Back-End Ratio:</strong> All monthly debt payments vs. income</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>What Affects How Much You Can Afford:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Annual Income:</strong> Higher income = larger loan qualification</li>
      <li><strong>Monthly Debts:</strong> Car payments, credit cards, student loans reduce capacity</li>
      <li><strong>Credit Score:</strong> Better scores get lower rates and higher loan amounts</li>
      <li><strong>Down Payment:</strong> More money down = lower loan amount needed</li>
      <li><strong>Interest Rates:</strong> Lower rates = more affordable monthly payments</li>
      <li><strong>Property Taxes:</strong> Vary significantly by location</li>
      <li><strong>PMI:</strong> Required if down payment is less than 20%</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Types of Income Lenders Consider:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Base Salary:</strong> W-2 employment income</li>
      <li><strong>Hourly Wages:</strong> Based on average hours worked</li>
      <li><strong>Commission:</strong> Usually averaged over 2 years</li>
      <li><strong>Bonuses:</strong> Must be consistent and documented</li>
      <li><strong>Self-Employment:</strong> Based on tax returns, often averaged</li>
      <li><strong>Investment Income:</strong> Dividends, interest, rental income</li>
      <li><strong>Social Security:</strong> Retirement or disability benefits</li>
      <li><strong>Pension:</strong> Retirement income from former employers</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Monthly Debts That Count Against You:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Credit Card Minimums:</strong> Based on current balances</li>
      <li><strong>Auto Loans:</strong> Car, truck, motorcycle payments</li>
      <li><strong>Student Loans:</strong> Federal and private education loans</li>
      <li><strong>Personal Loans:</strong> Unsecured debt payments</li>
      <li><strong>Other Mortgages:</strong> Investment property or second home</li>
      <li><strong>Child Support/Alimony:</strong> Court-ordered payments</li>
      <li><strong>Business Debt:</strong> If personally guaranteed</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Debts That Don't Count:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Utilities:</strong> Electric, gas, water, internet</li>
      <li><strong>Insurance:</strong> Health, auto, life insurance premiums</li>
      <li><strong>Cell Phone:</strong> Mobile phone plans</li>
      <li><strong>Groceries:</strong> Food and household expenses</li>
      <li><strong>Entertainment:</strong> Dining out, subscriptions</li>
      <li><strong>Savings:</strong> 401k contributions, emergency fund</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Down Payment Sources:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Personal Savings:</strong> Most common source</li>
      <li><strong>Gift Funds:</strong> From family members (must be documented)</li>
      <li><strong>401k Loan:</strong> Borrowing from retirement accounts</li>
      <li><strong>IRA Withdrawal:</strong> First-time buyer exemption</li>
      <li><strong>Down Payment Assistance:</strong> State and local programs</li>
      <li><strong>Employer Programs:</strong> Company relocation or home buying assistance</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Loan Program Differences:</h3>
    <p><strong>Conventional Loans:</strong></p>
    <ul>
      <li>Down payment: 3-20%</li>
      <li>DTI limit: Usually 43-45%</li>
      <li>Credit score: 620+ recommended</li>
      <li>PMI required if less than 20% down</li>
    </ul>

    <p><strong>FHA Loans:</strong></p>
    <ul>
      <li>Down payment: 3.5% minimum</li>
      <li>DTI limit: Up to 57% in some cases</li>
      <li>Credit score: 580+ (500+ with 10% down)</li>
      <li>MIP (mortgage insurance) required</li>
    </ul>

    <p><strong>VA Loans:</strong></p>
    <ul>
      <li>Down payment: 0% for qualified veterans</li>
      <li>DTI limit: Flexible, often higher than conventional</li>
      <li>No PMI required</li>
      <li>Funding fee applies (unless disabled veteran)</li>
    </ul>

    <p><strong>USDA Loans:</strong></p>
    <ul>
      <li>Down payment: 0% for eligible rural areas</li>
      <li>DTI limit: 41% typically</li>
      <li>Income limits apply based on area</li>
      <li>Upfront and annual fees required</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Credit Score Impact on Affordability:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>760-850:</strong> Best rates, highest loan amounts</li>
      <li><strong>700-759:</strong> Good rates, strong approval odds</li>
      <li><strong>660-699:</strong> Fair rates, may need larger down payment</li>
      <li><strong>620-659:</strong> Higher rates, limited program options</li>
      <li><strong>580-619:</strong> FHA loans possible, higher costs</li>
      <li><strong>Below 580:</strong> Very limited options, significant down payment required</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Regional Cost Considerations:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Property Taxes:</strong> 0.3% - 2.4% annually depending on state</li>
      <li><strong>Home Insurance:</strong> $800 - $3,000+ annually based on location and risks</li>
      <li><strong>HOA Fees:</strong> $50 - $500+ monthly in planned communities</li>
      <li><strong>Maintenance Costs:</strong> 1-2% of home value annually</li>
      <li><strong>Utilities:</strong> Varies significantly by climate and home size</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Pre-Approval vs. Pre-Qualification:</h3>
    <p><strong>Pre-Qualification:</strong></p>
    <ul>
      <li>Based on self-reported information</li>
      <li>Quick estimate of borrowing capacity</li>
      <li>No credit check or documentation required</li>
      <li>Not a commitment from lender</li>
    </ul>

    <p><strong>Pre-Approval:</strong></p>
    <ul>
      <li>Full application with documentation</li>
      <li>Credit check and income verification</li>
      <li>Conditional commitment from lender</li>
      <li>Stronger position when making offers</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Steps After Determining Affordability:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Improve Credit Score:</strong> Pay down debts, check credit reports</li>
      <li><strong>Save for Down Payment:</strong> Aim for 20% to avoid PMI</li>
      <li><strong>Reduce Monthly Debts:</strong> Pay off credit cards and loans</li>
      <li><strong>Shop for Rates:</strong> Compare offers from multiple lenders</li>
      <li><strong>Get Pre-Approved:</strong> Obtain official loan approval</li>
      <li><strong>Factor in All Costs:</strong> Include closing costs, moving, and immediate repairs</li>
    </ul>

    <p>Remember that just because you can afford a certain amount doesn't mean you should spend it all. Leave room in your budget for emergencies, savings, and other financial goals.</p>
